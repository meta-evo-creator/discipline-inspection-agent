# Regulations of the Communist Party of China on Disciplinary Punishment (2023 Revision)

## Content

```
Part I — General Provisions

Chapter I — Overall Requirements and Scope of Application

Article 1
This Regulation is formulated in accordance with the Constitution of the Communist Party of China for the purposes of upholding the Party Constitution and other Party regulations, maintaining strict Party discipline, purifying Party organizations, safeguarding the democratic rights of Party members, educating Party members to observe discipline and the law, preserving the unity and solidarity of the Party, and ensuring the implementation and execution of the Party's theories, lines, principles, policies, resolutions, and the national laws and regulations.

Article 2
The construction of Party discipline must adhere to the guidance of Marxism-Leninism, Mao Zedong Thought, Deng Xiaoping Theory, the important thought of Three Represents, the Scientific Outlook on Development, and Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era; adhere to and strengthen the Party's overall leadership; resolutely uphold General Secretary Xi Jinping's core position on the Central Committee and in the Party as a whole; resolutely uphold the authority and centralized, unified leadership of the Central Committee with Comrade Xi Jinping at its core; carry forward the great founding spirit of the Party; persist in self-reform; implement the strategic policy of comprehensively and rigorously governing the Party; implement the general requirements for Party building in the new era; promote the resolution of unique problems facing a large Party; improve the system for comprehensively and rigorously governing the Party; comprehensively strengthen Party discipline construction; and provide a strong disciplinary guarantee for comprehensively advancing the building of a strong country and the great cause of national rejuvenation through Chinese-style modernization.

Article 3
The Party Constitution is the most fundamental Party regulation and the overarching rule for governing the Party and managing its affairs. Party discipline is the code of conduct that must be observed by Party organizations at all levels and all Party members. Party organizations and Party members must remain true to the Party's founding mission, firmly establish political awareness, awareness of the overall situation, core awareness, and alignment awareness, always maintain firm confidence in the path, theory, system, and culture of socialism with Chinese characteristics, earnestly practice the correct views on power, performance, and career, consciously observe and uphold the Party Constitution, strictly implement and maintain Party discipline, voluntarily submit to Party discipline constraints, and exemplary observe national laws and regulations.

Article 4
The work of Party disciplinary punishment shall adhere to the following principles:

(1) Uphold the principle that the Party must supervise itself and govern itself comprehensively and rigorously. Maintain the strict tone, strict measures, and strict atmosphere over the long term; strengthen education, management, and supervision of Party organizations at all levels and all Party members; give priority to discipline; identify and address issues early, taking preventive measures.

(2) Equality before Party discipline. Party organizations and Party members who violate Party discipline must have discipline enforced seriously and impartially. No Party organization or Party member within the Party may be exempt from disciplinary constraints.

(3) Seek truth from facts. In handling violations of Party discipline by Party organizations and Party members, facts shall serve as the basis, the Party Constitution, other Party regulations, and national laws and regulations shall serve as the criteria, the enforcement of discipline and law shall be integrated, the nature of the conduct shall be accurately determined, distinctions shall be made among different circumstances, and appropriate handling shall be applied.

(4) Democratic centralism. The imposition of Party disciplinary punishment shall, in accordance with prescribed procedures, be decided through collective discussion by the Party organization. No individual or minority may make a decision or grant approval without authorization. Decisions made by higher-level Party organizations on the handling of Party organizations or Party members that violate Party discipline must be implemented by lower-level Party organizations.

(5) Learn from past mistakes to avoid future ones, and cure the illness to save the patient. In handling Party organizations and Party members that violate Party discipline, punishment and education shall be combined, leniency and severity shall be applied appropriately.

Article 5
Deepen the application of the "four forms" of supervision and discipline enforcement; regularly carry out criticism and self-criticism; promptly conduct talks, reminders, criticism education, orders for inspection, and admonishment; let the "red face and perspiration" become the norm; make light disciplinary punishment and organizational adjustment the majority in the handling of disciplinary violations; make heavy disciplinary punishment and major position adjustments the minority; and make serious disciplinary violations involving suspected crimes and criminal prosecution the extreme minority.

Article 6
This Regulation applies to Party organizations and Party members who, having violated Party discipline, should be subject to disciplinary accountability.

Chapter II — Violations of Discipline and Disciplinary Punishment

Article 7
Any conduct by a Party organization or Party member that violates the Party Constitution or other Party regulations, violates national laws or regulations, violates the policies of the Party and the state, violates socialist morality, or harms the interests of the Party, the state, or the people, which according to provisions should be subject to disciplinary handling or punishment, must be investigated.

Priority shall be given to investigating and punishing those who, since the 18th National Congress of the Party, have not stopped or desisted from their misconduct; have concentrated and prominent leads with strong public complaints; have corruption cases where political and economic issues are interwoven; and have problems involving violations of the Central Committee's eight-point regulations on improving work style and maintaining close ties with the masses.

Article 8
The types of disciplinary punishment for Party members are:

(1) Warning;
(2) Serious Warning;
(3) Removal from Party Posts;
(4) Probation within the Party;
(5) Expulsion from the Party.

Article 9
For a Party organization that has violated Party discipline, the higher-level Party organization shall order it to submit a written review or issue a notice of criticism. For a Party organization that has seriously violated Party discipline and is itself unable to correct, the Party committee at the next higher level may, after investigation and verification and based on the severity of the circumstances, impose:

(1) Reorganization;
(2) Dissolution.

Article 10
Within one year after receiving a Warning punishment, and within one and a half years after receiving a Serious Warning punishment, a Party member shall not be promoted to a post within the Party or further used, and shall not be recommended to non-Party organizations for a post higher than his or her original post or further used.

Article 11
The punishment of Removal from Party Posts refers to the removal of the Party posts held by the disciplined Party member through Party elections or organizational appointment. If a Party member holds two or more posts within the Party, the Party organization shall, when making the disciplinary decision, specify whether all posts or one or several posts are to be removed. If it is decided to remove one post, the highest post held by the member must be removed. If it is decided to remove two or more posts, removal must start from the highest post and proceed in descending order. If the Party member holds a post in a non-Party organization, it shall be recommended that the non-Party organization remove the non-Party post.

For a Party member who has been removed from his or her post during case filing and investigation due to suspected violation of Party discipline, and who, after investigation, should be given the punishment of Removal from Party Posts according to this Regulation, the punishment of Removal from Party Posts shall be imposed based on his or her original post. For a person who should receive the punishment of Removal from Party Posts but does not hold any Party post, a Serious Warning shall be imposed. At the same time, if the person holds a post in a non-Party organization, it shall be recommended that the non-Party organization remove the non-Party post.

A Party member who has received the punishment of Removal from Party Posts, or who has received a Serious Warning in accordance with the preceding paragraph, shall not, within two years, hold any Party post or be recommended to non-Party organizations for a post equivalent to or higher than the original post.

Article 12
The punishment of Probation within the Party is divided into a period of one year or two years. For a Party member who has received one year of Probation within the Party and still does not meet the conditions for the restoration of Party membership rights upon the expiration of the probation period, the probation period shall be extended for one additional year. The maximum period of Probation within the Party shall not exceed two years.

During the period a Party member is under Probation within the Party, the member shall not have the right to vote, the right to stand for election, or the right to be elected. If the member truly shows repentance during the probation period, his or her Party membership rights shall be restored upon expiration. If the member persists in not correcting the conduct or if other disciplinary violations warranting disciplinary punishment are discovered, the member shall be expelled from the Party.

When a Party member receives the punishment of Probation within the Party, his or her Party posts are automatically removed. If the member holds a non-Party post, it shall be recommended that the non-Party organization remove the non-Party post. A Party member who has received the punishment of Probation within the Party shall not, within two years after the restoration of Party membership rights, hold any Party post or be recommended to non-Party organizations for a post equivalent to or higher than the original post.

Article 13
A Party member who has been expelled from the Party shall not be readmitted to the Party within five years, and shall not be recommended for a non-Party post equivalent to or higher than his or her original post. If there are other provisions prohibiting readmission to the Party, those provisions shall apply.

Article 14
If a Party cadre who has received a Party disciplinary punishment also requires organizational handling, the Party organization shall give organizational handling in accordance with regulations.

If a representative to a Party congress at any level receives a punishment of Probation within the Party or above, the Party organization shall terminate his or her representative status.

Article 15
Members of the leading body of a Party organization that has been subject to reorganization shall be automatically removed from their posts, except for those who should receive a punishment of Removal from Party Posts or above.

Article 16
Party members in a Party organization that has been dissolved shall be reviewed individually. Among them, those who meet the qualifications for Party membership shall be re-registered and participate in Party life in a new organization. Those who do not meet the qualifications for Party membership shall receive education and be given a deadline for correction. If, after education, no change occurs, the member shall be persuaded to withdraw or be removed from the membership roll. If disciplinary violations are found, those shall be investigated in accordance with regulations.

Chapter III — Rules for the Application of Disciplinary Punishment

Article 17
Under any of the following circumstances, a lighter or mitigated punishment may be imposed:

(1) Voluntarily confessing to one's own issues that should be subject to Party disciplinary punishment;
(2) Cooperating with verification and investigation work during organizational talks, correspondence inquiries, preliminary verification, or case filing and investigation, and truthfully explaining one's own disciplinary or illegal facts;
(3) Reporting issues involving co-defendants or other persons that should be subject to Party disciplinary punishment or legal prosecution, which are verified as true, or having other meritorious conduct;
(4) Voluntarily mitigating losses, eliminating adverse effects, or effectively preventing the occurrence of harmful consequences;
(5) Voluntarily surrendering or refunding the gains obtained from disciplinary violations;
(6) Other circumstances provided for in Party regulations for a lighter or mitigated punishment.

Article 18
Based on the special circumstances of a case, a mitigated punishment outside the range specified in this Regulation may also be imposed on a Party member who has committed a disciplinary violation, provided that the decision is made by the Central Commission for Discipline Inspection, or by a provincial (ministerial)-level disciplinary inspection commission (excluding those in deputy-provincial-level cities) and reported to the Central Commission for Discipline Inspection for approval.

Article 19
For a Party member whose violation of Party discipline should be subject to a Warning or Serious Warning, but who has one of the circumstances specified in Article 17 of this Regulation or where otherwise provided in the Specific Provisions of this Regulation, criticism education, an order for inspection, admonishment, or organizational handling may be imposed, and Party disciplinary punishment may be exempted. Where a Party member who has violated discipline is exempted from punishment, a written conclusion shall be made.

If a Party member exhibits incipient or tendency issues in work style and discipline, or commits minor disciplinary violations, talks and reminders, criticism education, orders for inspection, etc., may be given, or admonishment may be imposed, without imposing Party disciplinary punishment.

If a Party member's conduct causes loss or consequences, but such loss or consequences are not caused by intention or negligence but rather by force majeure or other reasons, Party disciplinary accountability shall not be pursued.

Article 20
Under any of the following circumstances, a heavier or aggravated punishment shall be imposed:

(1) Compelling or instigating others to violate discipline;
(2) Refusing to surrender or refund the gains obtained from disciplinary violations;
(3) Having been punished for a disciplinary violation and then intentionally violating discipline again, warranting another Party disciplinary punishment;
(4) Having been punished for a disciplinary violation and then being found to have other issues that occurred before the punishment and were not confessed, which warrant Party disciplinary punishment;
(5) Other circumstances provided for in Party regulations for a heavier or aggravated punishment.

Article 21
If a Party member receives another Party disciplinary punishment during the period of influence of a prior Party disciplinary punishment, the period of influence shall be the sum of the unexpired period of the original punishment and the period of influence of the new punishment.

Article 22
A lighter punishment refers to imposing a lighter punishment within the range of punishment that should be imposed for the disciplinary violation as specified in this Regulation.

A heavier punishment refers to imposing a heavier punishment within the range of punishment that should be imposed for the disciplinary violation as specified in this Regulation.

Article 23
A mitigated punishment refers to imposing a punishment one grade lower than the range of punishment that should be imposed for the disciplinary violation as specified in this Regulation.

An aggravated punishment refers to imposing a punishment one grade higher than the range of punishment that should be imposed for the disciplinary violation as specified in this Regulation.

For disciplinary violations that have only one grade of punishment under this Regulation — Expulsion from the Party — the provisions on mitigated punishment in the first paragraph shall not apply.

Article 24
If a person commits two or more types of disciplinary violations specified in this Regulation that should each be subject to Party disciplinary punishment, the punishments shall be combined, and the punishment shall be imposed at one grade higher than the highest applicable punishment among the multiple types of disciplinary violations. If one of the types of disciplinary violations should be subject to Expulsion from the Party, then Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 25
If one disciplinary violation simultaneously violates two or more provisions of this Regulation, it shall be characterized and handled according to the provision with the heavier punishment.

If all the constituent elements of the disciplinary violation in one provision are fully contained within another provision, and there is inconsistency between the special provision and the general provision, the special provision shall apply.

Article 26
When two or more persons intentionally violate discipline together, the leader shall be given a heavier punishment, unless otherwise provided in this Regulation; other members shall be given punishments separately based on the role they played and the responsibility they bore in the joint disciplinary violation.

For joint disciplinary violations in economic matters, punishments shall be imposed separately based on each individual's participation amount and the role played. For the leader of a joint disciplinary violation, if the circumstances are serious, the punishment shall be imposed based on the total amount of the joint disciplinary violation.

A person who instigates others to violate discipline shall be held accountable for Party disciplinary responsibility based on the role he or she played in the joint disciplinary violation.

Article 27
If the leading body of a Party organization collectively makes a decision to violate Party discipline or commits other acts that violate Party discipline, members who share a common intent shall be treated as having committed a joint disciplinary violation; members who negligently committed disciplinary violations shall be punished separately based on the role they played and the responsibility they bore in the collective disciplinary violation.

Chapter IV — Disciplinary Punishment of Party Members Who Have Committed Illegal or Criminal Acts

Article 28
Party members who have committed illegal or criminal acts shall be given Party disciplinary punishment in accordance with regulations, achieving an organic integration of the application of discipline and law, and ensuring that Party disciplinary punishment and administrative disciplinary punishment correspond appropriately.

Article 29
If, during disciplinary review, a Party organization discovers that a Party member has engaged in conduct that violates the law and is suspected of committing a crime, such as corruption, bribery, abuse of power, dereliction of duty, rent-seeking, transmission of benefits, engaging in fraud for personal gain, or wasting state assets, the punishment of Removal from Party Posts, Probation within the Party, or Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 30
If, during disciplinary review, a Party organization discovers that a Party member has committed conduct specified in the Criminal Law, which does not constitute a crime but requires Party disciplinary accountability, or has committed other illegal acts that disrupt the socialist market economic order or violate public security management, thereby harming the interests of the Party, the state, or the people, a Warning up to Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed based on the specific circumstances.

If a Party member violates state financial and economic discipline, having illegal conduct in financial activities such as public fund revenue and expenditure, tax management, state asset management, government procurement management, financial management, and financial accounting management, the preceding paragraph shall apply.

If a Party member engages in conduct such as prostitution or the ingestion or injection of drugs, which results in the loss of the qualifications for Party membership and seriously damages the Party's image, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 31
If, during disciplinary review, a Party organization discovers that a Party member has committed a serious disciplinary violation and is suspected of an illegal or criminal act, in principle, the decision on Party disciplinary punishment shall be made first, and after the supervisory organ imposes an administrative disciplinary punishment in accordance with regulations, or after the appointing authority (unit) imposes a punishment, the case shall be transferred to the relevant state organ for handling according to law.

Article 32
If a Party member is subjected to detention or arrest according to law, the Party organization shall suspend his or her rights as a Party member, including the right to vote, the right to stand for election, and the right to be elected, in accordance with the management authority. If, based on the outcome of the supervisory organ or judicial authority, the Party member's rights can be restored, they shall be restored promptly.

Article 33
If a Party member commits a crime with minor circumstances and the People's Procuratorate makes a decision not to prosecute according to law, or if the People's Court renders a guilty verdict but exempts the person from criminal punishment according to law, the punishment of Removal from Party Posts, Probation within the Party, or Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

If a Party member commits a crime and is sentenced solely to a fine, the preceding paragraph shall apply.

Article 34
If a Party member commits a crime under any of the following circumstances, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed:

(1) Sentenced to a principal penalty as prescribed by the Criminal Law for an intentional crime (including where the sentence is suspended);
(2) Sentenced solely or additionally to deprivation of political rights;
(3) Sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of three years or more (excluding three years) for a negligent crime.

If sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of less than three years or to criminal detention or public surveillance for a negligent crime, Expulsion from the Party shall generally be imposed. In individual cases where Expulsion from the Party may not be imposed, approval shall be sought from the Party organization at the next higher level above the one responsible, in accordance with the provisions on the authority to approve the punishment of Party members who have violated discipline.

Article 35
If a Party member is subject to criminal liability according to law, the Party organization shall, based on the effective judgment, ruling, or decision of the judicial authority, as well as the facts, nature, and circumstances determined therein, impose Party disciplinary punishment in accordance with this Regulation. If the person is a public official, the supervisory organ shall impose a corresponding administrative disciplinary punishment, or the appointing authority (unit) shall impose a corresponding punishment.

If a Party member receives an administrative disciplinary punishment, a punishment imposed by the appointing authority (unit), or an administrative penalty according to law, and Party disciplinary accountability should be pursued, the Party organization may, based on the facts, nature, and circumstances determined in the effective disciplinary or administrative penalty decision and after verification, impose a corresponding Party disciplinary punishment or organizational handling in accordance with regulations. If the Party member has received a punishment of removal from post or above according to law, a punishment of Removal from Party Posts or above shall be imposed in accordance with this Regulation.

If a Party member violates national laws, regulations, or the rules and regulations of enterprises, institutions, or other social organizations, and receives other punishments, and Party disciplinary accountability should be pursued, the Party organization shall, after verifying the facts, nature, and circumstances determined by the relevant authorities, impose a corresponding Party disciplinary punishment or organizational handling in accordance with regulations.

Where, after the Party organization has made a decision on Party disciplinary punishment or organizational handling, the supervisory organ, judicial authority, or administrative authority legally changes the original effective judgment, ruling, decision, etc., and such change has an impact on the original Party disciplinary punishment or organizational handling decision, the Party organization shall make a new corresponding handling based on the revised effective judgment, ruling, decision, etc.

Chapter V — Miscellaneous Provisions

Article 36
If a probationary Party member violates Party discipline, and the circumstances are minor such that the probationary Party member's status can be retained, the Party organization shall criticize and educate the member or extend the probationary period. If the circumstances are serious, the member's status as a probationary Party member shall be revoked.

Article 37
For a Party member whose whereabouts are unknown after violating discipline, handling shall be determined based on the circumstances:

(1) If the member has engaged in serious disciplinary violations and should be expelled from the Party, the Party organization shall make a decision to expel the member;
(2) Except as provided in the preceding paragraph, if the member's whereabouts remain unknown for more than six months, the Party organization shall remove the member from the membership roll in accordance with the Party Constitution.

Article 38
If a Party member who has violated discipline dies before the Party organization makes a disciplinary decision, or if it is discovered after death that the member had engaged in serious disciplinary violations, and if the member should have been expelled from the Party, the member shall be posthumously expelled from the Party. If the member should have received a punishment of Probation within the Party or below, a written conclusion of the disciplinary violation and corresponding handling shall be made.

Article 39
The distinction of responsible persons in connection with disciplinary violations:

(1) "Person directly responsible" refers to a Party member or leading Party cadre who, within the scope of his or her responsibilities, fails to perform or fails to correctly perform his or her duties, and whose conduct plays a decisive role in causing the loss or consequences;
(2) "Person with primary leadership responsibility" refers to a leading Party cadre who, within the scope of his or her responsibilities, fails to perform or fails to correctly perform his or her duties regarding the work under his or her charge, and bears direct leadership responsibility for the loss or consequences;
(3) "Person with important leadership responsibility" refers to a leading Party cadre who, within the scope of his or her responsibilities, fails to perform or fails to correctly perform his or her duties regarding the work he or she should manage or participated in deciding, and bears secondary leadership responsibility for the loss or consequences.

As used in this Regulation, "leading responsible person" includes persons with primary leadership responsibility and persons with important leadership responsibility.

Article 40
As used in this Regulation, "voluntarily confessing" refers to a Party member suspected of disciplinary violations voluntarily revealing his or her own issues to the relevant organization before organizational talks, correspondence inquiries, or preliminary verification, or revealing issues that the organization has not yet learned about during the period of talks, correspondence inquiries, preliminary verification, or case filing and investigation.

Article 41
If a Party cadre who holds a rank or a standalone rank sequence violates Party discipline and receives a punishment, and his or her rank or standalone rank sequence needs to be adjusted, the provisions of this Regulation concerning non-Party posts shall apply by reference.

Article 42
When calculating economic losses, all property losses actually incurred at the time of case filing shall be calculated, including various expenses paid to mitigate the losses caused by the disciplinary violation. Economic losses that continue to be incurred from the time of case filing until the time of handling shall also be included in the calculation.

Article 43
Economic benefits obtained from disciplinary violations shall be confiscated or ordered to be refunded. Gains voluntarily surrendered from disciplinary violations and compensation for economic losses shall be accepted, and confiscated or returned to the relevant entities or individuals in accordance with regulations.

For other benefits obtained from disciplinary violations, such as positions, ranks, professional titles, academic qualifications, degrees, awards, rewards, and qualifications, the disciplinary inspection organ handling the case, or the disciplinary inspection organ at the next higher level, shall recommend that the relevant organization, department, or unit make corrections in accordance with regulations.

For Party members handled in accordance with Articles 37 and 38 of this Regulation, if it is confirmed through investigation that they obtained benefits from the disciplinary violations they committed, such benefits shall be handled in accordance with this Article.

Article 44
After a decision on Party disciplinary punishment is made, it shall be announced to all Party members of the basic-level Party organization to which the disciplined Party member belongs, and to the disciplined Party member personally, within one month. If the disciplined person is a member of the leading body, the decision shall also be announced to the leading body of the Party organization to which the person belongs. The materials concerning the punishment decision shall be placed in the disciplined person's file in accordance with the cadre management authority and organizational relationship. If the punishment imposed is Removal from Party Posts or above, corresponding changes to the person's post, salary, work, and other relevant benefits shall be made within one month. If the removal or adjustment of the person's non-Party posts is involved, it shall be recommended that the non-Party organization promptly remove or adjust such non-Party posts. Under special circumstances, with the approval of the organization that made or approved the punishment decision, the processing period may be appropriately extended. The maximum processing period shall not exceed six months.

Article 45
The organ implementing the Party disciplinary punishment decision, or the unit to which the disciplined Party member belongs, shall, within six months, report the implementation of the punishment decision to the organ that made or approved the punishment decision.

If a Party member disagrees with the Party disciplinary punishment imposed, he or she may appeal in accordance with the Party Constitution and relevant provisions.

Article 46
Once the period of influence for a Party member's disciplinary punishment has expired, the Party organization does not need to cancel the punishment.

Article 47
As used in this Regulation, "up to" and "down to" include the referenced grade or number itself, unless otherwise specified.

Article 48
The General Provisions of this Regulation shall apply to other Party regulations that contain provisions on Party disciplinary punishment, except where other Party regulations promulgated or approved for promulgation by the Central Committee of the CPC provide otherwise.

Part II — Specific Provisions

Chapter VI — Punishment for Acts Violating Political Discipline

Article 49
Where a person disagrees with the Central Committee on matters of major principle and has actual remarks, conduct, or causes adverse consequences, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; where the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 50
Where a person, through the internet, radio, television, newspapers, periodicals, leaflets, books, or by using lectures, forums, seminars, symposiums, or other means, publicly publishes articles, speeches, declarations, statements, etc., that uphold the stance of bourgeois liberalization, oppose the Four Cardinal Principles, or oppose the Party's reform and opening-up decisions, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Where articles, speeches, declarations, statements, etc., listed in the preceding paragraph are published, broadcast, aired, printed, or disseminated, or where convenience is provided for such conduct, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall be given a Serious Warning or Removal from Party Posts; where the circumstances are serious, Probation within the Party or Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 51
Where a person, through the internet, radio, television, newspapers, periodicals, leaflets, books, or by using lectures, forums, seminars, symposiums, or other means, engages in any of the following conduct, if the circumstances are relatively minor, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed:

(1) Publicly publishing articles, speeches, declarations, statements, etc., that violate the Four Cardinal Principles, violate or distort the Party's reform and opening-up decisions, or have other serious political problems;
(2) Making reckless comments on the major policies of the Central Committee, undermining the centralized unity of the Party;
(3) Vilifying the image of the Party and the state, or slandering or defaming Party and state leaders, heroes, or role models, or distorting the history of the Party, the history of the People's Republic of China, or the history of the People's Army.

Where the content listed in the preceding paragraph is published, broadcast, aired, printed, or disseminated, or where convenience is provided for such conduct, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall be given a Serious Warning or Removal from Party Posts; where the circumstances are serious, Probation within the Party or Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 52
Where a person produces, sells, or disseminates newspapers, periodicals, books, audio-visual products, electronic readings, or online texts, images, audio, or video materials containing any of the content listed in Articles 50 or 51, if the circumstances are relatively minor, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Where a person privately carries or sends by post into or out of the country newspapers, periodicals, books, audio-visual products, electronic readings, etc., containing any of the content listed in Articles 50 or 51, if the circumstances are relatively serious, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts, Probation within the Party, or Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Where a person privately reads, browses, or listens to newspapers, periodicals, books, audio-visual products, electronic readings, or online texts, images, audio, or video materials containing any of the content listed in Articles 50 or 51, if the circumstances are serious, a Warning, Serious Warning, or Removal from Party Posts shall be imposed.

Article 53
Where a person organizes a secret faction or engages in other activities aimed at splitting the Party within the Party, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Where a person participates in a secret faction or participates in other activities aimed at splitting the Party, Probation within the Party or Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 54
Where a person engages in non-organizational activities within the Party such as forming cliques, building personal networks, establishing factions, engaging in political patronage, or cultivating personal influence, or seeks political capital through activities such as exchanging benefits or building personal momentum, a Serious Warning or Removal from Party Posts shall be imposed; where this leads to the deterioration of the political ecosystem of the region, department, or unit, Probation within the Party or Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 55
Where a person engages in speculative activities, cultivates relations with political fraudsters, or is used by political fraudsters, a Serious Warning or Removal from Party Posts shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Probation within the Party or Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Where a person acts as a political fraudster, Removal from Party Posts, Probation within the Party, or Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 56
Where a leading Party cadre, in the locality under his or her jurisdiction or the department under his or her charge, acts willfully, engages in mountaintop-ism, refuses to implement the major policies established by the Central Committee, or even goes against the Central Committee, Removal from Party Posts, Probation within the Party, or Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Where a person merely pays lip-service to the implementation of the Central Committee's decisions and deployments without actually implementing them, or implements the Central Committee's decisions and deployments without resolve, discounting or distorting them, thereby causing adverse political consequences or serious consequences, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts, Probation within the Party, or Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Where a person disregards the overall interests of the Party and the state and engages in departmental or local protectionism, the preceding paragraph shall apply.

Article 57
Where a leading Party cadre has a distorted view on performance, violates the new development philosophy, deviates from the requirements of high-quality development, and causes significant losses to the interests of the Party, the state, and the people, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; where the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Where a person engages in "image projects" or "vanity projects" that waste resources and burden the people, a heavier or aggravated punishment shall be imposed.

Article 58
Where a person is disloyal and dishonest toward the Party, says one thing but means another, complies in public but opposes in private, deceives superiors and subordinates, engages in double-dealing, and acts as a two-faced person, thereby causing adverse political influence, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; where the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 59
Where a person fabricates, spreads, or disseminates political rumors, undermining the unity and solidarity of the Party, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; where the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Where a person has poor political character, engages in anonymous false accusations, intentionally slanders others, or fabricates other rumors, causing damage or adverse influence, the preceding paragraph shall apply.

Article 60
Where a person, without authorization, makes decisions or publicly expresses opinions on major policy issues that should be decided by the Central Committee, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall be given a Serious Warning or Removal from Party Posts; where the circumstances are serious, Probation within the Party or Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 61
Where a person fails to request instructions or report major matters to the organization in accordance with relevant regulations, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall, if the circumstances are relatively serious, be given a Warning or Serious Warning; if the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed.

Article 62
Where a person interferes with inspection or disciplinary inspection tour work, or fails to implement the rectification requirements from inspection or disciplinary inspection tour, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall, if the circumstances are relatively minor, be given a Warning or Serious Warning; if the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 63
Where a person resists organizational review by engaging in any of the following acts, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; where the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed:

(1) Colluding to make consistent statements or forging, destroying, transferring, or concealing evidence;
(2) Preventing others from exposing, reporting, or providing evidence materials;
(3) Covering up co-defendants;
(4) Providing false information to the organization to conceal facts;
(5) Other acts of resisting organizational review.

Article 64
Where a person organizes or participates in assemblies, processions, demonstrations, or other activities opposing the Party's basic theory, basic line, basic policy, or major principles and policies, or by organizing lectures, forums, seminars, symposiums, or other means opposes the Party's basic theory, basic line, basic policy, or major principles and policies, thereby causing serious adverse influence, the planners, organizers, and core members shall be expelled from the Party.

For other participants, or those who support the above-mentioned activities by providing information, materials, funds, or venues, if the circumstances are relatively minor, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

For those who participated without understanding the true situation and who show genuine repentance after criticism and education, disciplinary punishment may be exempted or not imposed.

Where a person participates in other assemblies, processions, or demonstrations without organizational approval, if the circumstances are relatively minor, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 65
Where a person organizes or participates in organizations aimed at opposing the Party's leadership, opposing the socialist system, or being hostile to the government, the planners, organizers, and core members shall be expelled from the Party.

For other participants, if the circumstances are relatively minor, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 66
Where a person organizes or participates in superstitious sects or cult organizations, the planners, organizers, and core members shall be expelled from the Party.

For other participants, if the circumstances are relatively minor, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

For those who participated without understanding the true situation and who show genuine repentance after criticism and education, disciplinary punishment may be exempted or not imposed.

Article 67
Where a person engages in or participates in activities that provoke and undermine ethnic relations, create incidents, or participate in separatist activities, the planners, organizers, and core members shall be expelled from the Party.

For other participants, if the circumstances are relatively minor, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

For those who participated without understanding the true situation and who show genuine repentance after criticism and education, disciplinary punishment may be exempted or not imposed.

Where there are other acts that violate the Party's and the state's ethnic policies, if the circumstances are relatively minor, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 68
Where a person organizes or uses religious activities to oppose the Party's theories, lines, principles, policies, and resolutions, or to undermine ethnic unity, the planners, organizers, and core members shall be expelled from the Party.

For other participants, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

For those who participated without understanding the true situation and who show genuine repentance after criticism and education, disciplinary punishment may be exempted or not imposed.

Where there are other acts that violate the Party's and the state's religious policies, if the circumstances are relatively minor, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 69
For Party members who hold religious beliefs, ideological education shall be strengthened, and they shall be required to correct within a specified period. If, after help and education from the Party organization, they still do not change, they shall be advised to withdraw from the Party. If, after being advised, they do not withdraw, they shall be removed from the membership roll. If they participate in using religion to incite activities, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 70
Where a person organizes superstitious activities, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Where a person participates in superstitious activities or engages in superstitious activities as an individual, causing adverse influence, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; where the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

For those who participated without understanding the true situation and who show genuine repentance after criticism and education, disciplinary punishment may be exempted or not imposed.

Article 71
Where a person organizes or uses clan forces to confront the Party and the government, obstructs the implementation of the Party's and the state's principles, policies, and decisions and deployments, or undermines the construction of basic-level Party organizations, the planners, organizers, and core members shall be expelled from the Party.

For other participants, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

For those who participated without understanding the true situation and who show genuine repentance after criticism and education, disciplinary punishment may be exempted or not imposed.

Article 72
Where a person applies for political asylum outside the country (territory) or at a foreign embassy or consulate in China, or flees outside the country (territory) or to a foreign embassy or consulate in China after violating discipline, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Where a person publicly publishes articles, speeches, declarations, statements, etc., opposing the Party and the government outside the country (territory), the preceding paragraph shall apply.

Where a person intentionally provides convenience for the above-mentioned conduct, Probation within the Party or Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 73
Where, in foreign-related activities, a person's statements or conduct cause adverse political influence and harm the dignity and interests of the Party and the state, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 74
Where a person fails to fulfill the main responsibility or supervisory responsibility for comprehensively and rigorously governing the Party, or performs such responsibilities in a lackluster manner, causing serious damage or serious adverse influence to the Party organization, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall be given a Warning or Serious Warning; where the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed.

Article 75
Where a leading Party cadre fails to report, resist, or fight against erroneous thoughts and conduct that violate political discipline and political rules, allowing such conduct to go unchecked and practicing unprincipled harmony, thereby causing adverse influence, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed.

Article 76
Where a person violates the Party's fine traditions, work conventions, and other Party rules, causing adverse political influence or serious consequences, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; where the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Chapter VII — Punishment for Acts Violating Organizational Discipline

Article 77
Where a person violates the principle of democratic centralism by engaging in any of the following acts, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed:

(1) Refusing to implement or altering without authorization major decisions made by the Party organization;
(2) Violating the rules of procedure by having an individual or a minority decide on major issues;
(3) Intentionally evading collective decision-making on major matters, important cadre appointments, important project arrangements, and the use of large sums of funds;
(4) Using the pretext of collective decision-making to collectively violate regulations.

Article 78
Where a lower-level Party organization refuses to implement or alters without authorization a decision of a higher-level Party organization, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall be given a Warning or Serious Warning; where the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed.

Article 79
Where a person refuses to implement decisions of the Party organization regarding assignment, transfer, exchange, etc., a Warning, Serious Warning, or Removal from Party Posts shall be imposed.

During a special period or under emergency circumstances, where a person refuses to implement the above-mentioned decisions of the Party organization, Probation within the Party or Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 80
Where, in the Party organization's disciplinary review, a Party member who has the legal and regulatory obligation to testify refuses to testify or intentionally provides false information, if the circumstances are relatively serious, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts, Probation within the Party, or Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 81
Any of the following acts, if the circumstances are relatively serious, shall result in a Warning or Serious Warning:

(1) Violating regulations on reporting personal matters by concealing and failing to report;
(2) Failing to truthfully explain issues to the organization during organizational talks or correspondence inquiries;
(3) Failing to report one's whereabouts as required, or failing to truthfully report one's whereabouts;
(4) Failing to truthfully fill in personal archival materials.

Where a person commits the act described in item (2) of the preceding paragraph and at the same time provides false information to the organization to conceal facts, Article 63 of this Regulation shall apply.

Where a person alters or forges personal archival materials, a Serious Warning shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed.

Where a person conceals serious mistakes made before joining the Party, the person shall generally be removed from the membership roll. For those who have been Party members for many years with consistently good performance or who have made outstanding contributions in their work, a Serious Warning, Removal from Party Posts, or Probation within the Party shall be imposed.

Article 82
Where a leading Party cadre, in violation of relevant regulations, organizes or participates in spontaneously established fraternal associations, alumni associations, or comrades-in-arms associations, if the circumstances are serious, a Warning, Serious Warning, or Removal from Party Posts shall be imposed.

Article 83
Any of the following acts shall result in a Warning or Serious Warning; where the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed:

(1) Engaging in non-organizational activities such as vote canvassing or vote lobbying in democratic recommendations, democratic evaluations, organizational assessments, or Party elections;
(2) Engaging in non-organizational activities in voting or election activities prescribed by law, in violation of organizational principles, or organizing, instigating, or inducing others to vote or cast ballots;
(3) Engaging in other activities in elections that violate the Party Constitution, other Party regulations, or relevant charters.

Where a person engages in organized vote-buying or uses public funds for vote-buying, a heavier or aggravated punishment shall be imposed.

Article 84
Where, in the selection and appointment of cadres, a person engages in acts that violate the regulations on cadre selection and appointment, such as appointing people by favoritism, excluding those with different views, making promises of official positions, engaging in improper intervention, seeking official positions, or making rash promotions or adjustments of cadres, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall, if the circumstances are relatively minor, be given a Warning or Serious Warning; if the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Where employing the wrong person due to negligence in assessment causes serious consequences, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall be dealt with in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

Article 85
Where, in promoting the work of enabling cadres to be able to move up and down, a person engages in the practice of being a "good guy" and commits any of the following acts, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall, if the circumstances are relatively serious, be given a Warning or Serious Warning; if the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed:

(1) Using Party disciplinary or administrative punishment to evade organizational adjustment;
(2) Substituting organizational adjustment for Party disciplinary or administrative punishment;
(3) Other acts of making decisions that deal with minor issues while avoiding major ones.

Article 86
Where, in the recruitment, assessment, appointment and promotion of posts and ranks, evaluation and appointment of professional titles, honors and commendations, conferral of academic titles, and conscription and resettlement of retired military personnel, a person conceals or distorts facts, or uses his or her authority or position influence to seek benefits for himself or herself or others in violation of relevant regulations, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; where the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Where a person engages in falsification to defraud positions, ranks, professional titles, treatment, qualifications, academic credentials, degrees, honors, titles, or other benefits, the preceding paragraph shall apply.

Article 87
Where a person infringes upon a Party member's right to vote, right to stand for election, or right to be elected, if the circumstances are relatively serious, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts shall be imposed.

Where a person uses coercion, threats, deception, or inducement to obstruct a Party member from independently exercising his or her right to vote, right to stand for election, or right to be elected, Removal from Party Posts, Probation within the Party, or Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 88
Any of the following acts, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall be given a Warning or Serious Warning; where the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed:

(1) Obstructing or suppressing criticism, reports, or accusations; or privately suppressing or destroying materials related to criticism, reports, or accusations; or intentionally disclosing such materials to others;
(2) Suppressing a Party member's defense, representation, or testimony, causing adverse consequences;
(3) Suppressing a Party member's appeal, causing adverse consequences, or failing to handle a Party member's appeal in accordance with relevant regulations;
(4) Other acts infringing upon the rights of Party members, causing adverse consequences.

Where a person retaliates against critics, reporters, accusers, witnesses, or other persons, a heavier or aggravated punishment shall be imposed.

Article 89
Where a person, in violation of the Party Constitution and other Party regulations, uses deception or other means to develop a person who does not meet the conditions for Party membership into the Party, or issues a certificate of Party membership for a non-Party member, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall be given a Warning or Serious Warning; where the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts shall be imposed.

Where a person develops Party members in violation of the relevant procedures, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall be dealt with in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

Article 90
Where a person, in violation of relevant regulations, acquires foreign nationality, or obtains the right of permanent residence or long-term residence permit outside the country (territory), Removal from Party Posts, Probation within the Party, or Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 91
Where a person, in violation of relevant regulations, obtains a passport for private overseas travel, a permit to travel to and from Hong Kong or Macao, or enters or exits the country (border) without approval, if the circumstances are relatively minor, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Where a person, despite being approved for private overseas travel, changes the route without authorization, or fails to return within the time limit without justifiable cause, or otherwise exceeds the scope of approved travel, if the circumstances are relatively serious, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts shall be imposed.

Article 92
Where a Party member in a diplomatic mission abroad or a temporary overseas delegation (group) leaves the organization without authorization, or a Party member engaged in foreign affairs, classified communications, military, or similar work contacts or associates with foreign institutions or persons in violation of relevant regulations, a Warning, Serious Warning, or Removal from Party Posts shall be imposed.

Article 93
Where a Party member in a diplomatic mission abroad or a temporary overseas delegation (group) leaves the organization and absconds without authorization, but voluntarily returns within six months, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; where the absconding period exceeds six months, the member shall be treated as having voluntarily withdrawn from the Party and shall be removed from the membership roll.

Where a person intentionally provides convenience for others to leave the organization and abscond without authorization, a Warning, Serious Warning, or Removal from Party Posts shall be imposed.

Chapter VIII — Punishment for Acts Violating Integrity Discipline

Article 94
Leading Party cadres must correctly exercise the power conferred by the people, remain honest and upright, oppose the mentality of privilege and the phenomenon of privilege, and oppose any act of abusing power or seeking private gain.

Where a person uses his or her authority or position influence to seek benefits for others, and the person's spouse, children and their spouses, other relatives, or other persons with special relationships accept property from the other party, if the circumstances are relatively serious, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts, Probation within the Party, or Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 95
Where persons mutually use their respective authority or position influence to seek benefits for each other's spouses, children and their spouses, other relatives, staff in close proximity, or other persons with special relationships, engaging in power-for-power transactions, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; where the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 96
Where a person connives at or acquiesces in his or her spouse, children and their spouses, other relatives, staff in close proximity, or other persons with special relationships using the Party cadre's own authority or position influence to seek private gain, if the circumstances are relatively minor, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Where the spouse, children and their spouses, or other relatives or other persons with special relationships of a leading Party cadre receive remuneration without actually working, or although actually working, receive compensation that is clearly beyond the standard for comparable positions, and the leading Party cadre, being aware of this, fails to correct it, the preceding paragraph shall apply.

Article 97
Where a person accepts gifts, money, consumer cards (vouchers), negotiable securities, equities, or other financial products or property that could affect the impartial execution of official duties, if the circumstances are relatively minor, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Where a person accepts other property clearly exceeding the scope of normal reciprocal gift-giving, the preceding paragraph shall apply.

Article 98
Where a person gives gifts, money, consumer cards (vouchers), negotiable securities, equities, or other financial products or property to persons engaged in public service, or to their spouses, children and their spouses, other relatives, or other persons with special relationships, which clearly exceeds the scope of normal reciprocal gift-giving, if the circumstances are relatively serious, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed.

Where a person gives gifts in disguised form under the guise of lecture fees, project fees, consulting fees, etc., the preceding paragraph shall apply.

Article 99
Where a person borrows money, housing, vehicles, etc., from entities or individuals under management or service that could affect the impartial execution of official duties, if the circumstances are relatively serious, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts, Probation within the Party, or Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Where a person obtains substantial returns through private lending or other financial activities that could affect the impartial execution of official duties, the preceding paragraph shall apply.

Article 100
Where a person uses his or her authority or position influence to host wedding or funeral events, causing adverse influence, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts shall be imposed; where the person uses such events to accumulate wealth or commits other acts that infringe upon the interests of the state, the collective, or the people, a heavier or aggravated punishment shall be imposed, up to Expulsion from the Party.

Article 101
Where a person accepts or provides entertainment, such as banquets, or arrangements for activities such as tourism, fitness, or recreation, that could affect the impartial execution of official duties, if the circumstances are relatively serious, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed.

Article 102
Where a person, in violation of relevant regulations, obtains, holds, or actually uses various consumer cards (vouchers) such as fitness cards, club and association membership cards, golf cards, etc., or enters private clubs in violation of relevant regulations, if the circumstances are relatively serious, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed.

Article 103
Where a person, in violation of relevant regulations, engages in for-profit activities, committing any of the following acts, if the circumstances are relatively minor, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed:

(1) Engaging in business or running an enterprise;
(2) Holding shares or securities in non-listed companies (enterprises);
(3) Trading stocks or engaging in other securities investments;
(4) Engaging in paid intermediary activities;
(5) Registering a company outside the country (territory) or investing in shares;
(6) Other acts of engaging in for-profit activities in violation of relevant regulations.

Where a person uses information obtained from participating in work such as enterprise restructuring, private placements, merger and investment, or land use rights transfers to trade stocks, or uses his or her authority or position influence to obtain abnormal profits by purchasing trust products, funds, etc., the preceding paragraph shall apply.

Where a person, in violation of relevant regulations, holds a concurrent position in an economic organization, social organization, or other entity, or, although approved for a concurrent position, receives additional benefits such as compensation, bonuses, or allowances, the first paragraph shall apply.

Article 104
Where a person uses his or her authority or position influence to seek benefits for his or her spouse, children and their spouses, other relatives, or other persons with special relationships in areas such as approval and supervision, resource development, financial credit, bulk procurement, land use rights transfers, real estate development, project bidding, and public finance revenue and expenditure, if the circumstances are relatively minor, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Where a person uses his or her authority or position influence to provide assistance to his or her spouse, children and their spouses, other relatives, or other persons with special relationships in attracting deposits, marketing financial products, dealing in special resources such as famous local specialty products, etc., in order to seek benefits, the preceding paragraph shall apply.

Article 105
Where a person, after leaving office or retiring, in violation of relevant regulations, accepts employment in the region or business scope under his or her former post, or in enterprises or intermediary institutions directly related to his or her former work, or personally engages in for-profit activities directly related to the business under his or her former post or to his or her former work, if the circumstances are relatively minor, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Probation within the Party shall be imposed.

Where a leading Party cadre, after leaving office or retiring, in violation of relevant regulations, serves as an independent director, independent supervisor, or other position in a listed company or fund management company, if the circumstances are relatively minor, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Probation within the Party shall be imposed.

Article 106
Where a person, after leaving office or retiring, uses his or her former authority or position influence to seek benefits for his or her spouse, children and their spouses, other relatives, or other persons with special relationships in engaging in business activities, if the circumstances are relatively minor, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Where a person, after leaving office or retiring, uses his or her former authority or position influence to seek benefits for others, and the person's spouse, children and their spouses, other relatives, or other persons with special relationships accept property from the other party, if the circumstances are relatively serious, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts, Probation within the Party, or Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 107
Where the spouse, children, or their spouses of a leading Party cadre, in violation of relevant regulations, engage in business activities within the region or business scope under the leading Party cadre's jurisdiction that could affect the impartial execution of official duties, or commit other acts violating the prohibition provisions on engaging in business and running enterprises, the leading Party cadre shall make corrections in accordance with regulations; if the leading Party cadre refuses to correct, he or she shall resign from his or her current position, or the organization shall adjust his or her position; if the leading Party cadre does not resign from his or her current position or does not submit to organizational adjustment of position, Removal from Party Posts shall be imposed.

Article 108
Where a Party or state organ engages in business or runs an enterprise in violation of relevant regulations, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall be given a Warning or Serious Warning; where the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts shall be imposed.

Article 109
Where a leading Party cadre, in violation of the work and living security system, seeks special treatment for himself or herself, his or her spouse, children and their spouses, other relatives, staff in close proximity, or other persons with special relationships in areas such as transportation, medical care, or security, if the circumstances are relatively serious, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed.

Article 110
Where a person infringes upon the interests of the state or the collective in the allocation or purchase of housing, if the circumstances are relatively minor, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 111
Where a person, using his or her authority or position influence, misappropriates public or private property not under his or her management, or misappropriates public or private property by paying a nominal price, or receives services or uses labor without paying or by paying only a nominal fee, if the circumstances are relatively minor, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Where a person, using his or her authority or position influence, has expenses that should be paid personally by himself or herself, his or her spouse, children and their spouses, other relatives, staff in close proximity, or other persons with special relationships, paid or reimbursed by subordinate units, other units, or other persons, the preceding paragraph shall apply.

Article 112
Where a person, using his or her authority or position influence, and in violation of relevant regulations, occupies public property for personal use for more than six months, if the circumstances are relatively serious, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts shall be imposed.

Where a person occupies public property for for-profit activities, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; where the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Where a person lends public property to others for for-profit activities, the preceding paragraph shall apply.

Article 113
Where a person, in violation of relevant regulations, organizes or participates in banquets, entertainment, or fitness activities paid with public funds, or purchases or gives away gifts, consumer cards (vouchers), etc., with public funds, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall, if the circumstances are relatively minor, be given a Warning or Serious Warning; if the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 114
Where a person, in violation of relevant regulations, sets his or her own salary, or excessively distributes allowances, subsidies, bonuses, welfare, etc., the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall, if the circumstances are relatively minor, be given a Warning or Serious Warning; if the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 115
Any of the following acts, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall, if the circumstances are relatively minor, be given a Warning or Serious Warning; if the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed:

(1) Taking publicly funded trips, or disguised publicly funded trips under the pretext of study tours, investigation and research, employee recuperation, etc.;
(2) Changing the official itinerary to take advantage of the opportunity to travel;
(3) Participating in investigation activities organized by enterprises under management or subordinate units and taking the opportunity to travel.

Where a person takes disguised publicly funded overseas trips under the pretext of investigation, study, training, seminars, investment promotion, exhibitions, etc., the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall be dealt with in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

Article 116
Where a person, in violation of reception management regulations, exceeds standards or scope in receptions, or takes the opportunity to indulge in excessive eating and drinking, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall, if the circumstances are relatively serious, be given a Warning or Serious Warning; if the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts shall be imposed.

Article 117
Where a person, in violation of relevant regulations, allocates, purchases, replaces, decorates, or uses official vehicles, or commits other acts violating the management regulations on official vehicles, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall, if the circumstances are relatively serious, be given a Warning or Serious Warning; if the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed.

Article 118
Any of the following acts in violation of the management regulations on meetings and events, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall, if the circumstances are relatively serious, be given a Warning or Serious Warning; if the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts shall be imposed:

(1) Holding meetings in scenic and historic areas where meetings are prohibited;
(2) Deciding or approving the holding of various festivals, celebrations, or events;
(3) Other acts in violation of the management regulations on meetings and events.

Where a person holds without authorization activities for evaluation, assessment, and commendation, or for the creation of demonstration projects, or uses such activities to collect fees, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall be dealt with in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

Article 119
Any of the following acts in violation of the regulations on the management of office spaces, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall, if the circumstances are relatively serious, be given a Warning or Serious Warning; if the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts shall be imposed:

(1) Deciding or approving the construction or renovation of office buildings, training centers, or other such facilities;
(2) Allocating or using office spaces exceeding the standard;
(3) Renting or borrowing office spaces without approval;
(4) Using public funds to contract or occupy hotel rooms or other premises for personal use;
(5) Other acts in violation of the regulations on the management of office spaces.

Article 120
Where a person engages in power-for-sex transactions or gives property in exchange for sexual favors, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; where the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 121
Where there are other acts in violation of the provisions on integrity discipline, a Warning up to Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed based on the specific circumstances.

Chapter IX — Punishment for Acts Violating the Discipline of Mass Relations

Article 122
Any of the following acts, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall, if the circumstances are relatively minor, be given a Warning or Serious Warning; if the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed:

(1) Exceeding the standard or scope in raising funds or labor from the masses, or apportioning expenses, thereby increasing the burden on the masses;
(2) Unlawfully withholding or confiscating the money or property of the masses, or punishing the masses in violation of relevant regulations;
(3) Embezzling the property of the masses, or withholding money owed to the masses in violation of relevant regulations;
(4) Charging fees in violation of relevant regulations in management or service activities;
(5) Making things difficult for the masses, or demanding and accepting things from the masses when handling matters involving them;
(6) Other acts that infringe upon the interests of the masses.

Where the above-mentioned acts occur in the field of rural revitalization, a heavier or aggravated punishment shall be imposed.

Article 123
Where a person interferes with the decision-making power of entities regarding their production and operations, causing significant losses to the property of the masses, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall be given a Warning or Serious Warning; where the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed.

Article 124
Where, in the distribution of social security, social assistance, policy support, disaster relief, emergency aid funds or materials, etc., a person shows favoritism toward relatives or friends or is clearly unfair, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; where the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 125
Where a person uses clan or mafia-like forces to oppress the masses, or connives at activities involving organized crime and mafia-like forces, or acts as a "protective umbrella" for such forces, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 126
Any of the following acts, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall, if the circumstances are relatively serious, be given a Warning or Serious Warning; if the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed:

(1) Failing to promptly resolve issues concerning the production and living interests of the masses that can be resolved in accordance with policies or relevant regulations, being lazy, inactive, and inefficient, thereby causing adverse influence;
(2) Responding with apathy to legitimate demands of the masses that are consistent with policies, or passing the buck, thereby harming the relationship between the Party and the masses and between cadres and the masses;
(3) Treating the masses in a poor manner, being rough and rude, thereby causing adverse influence;
(4) Engaging in falsification, deceiving superiors and subordinates, thereby harming the interests of the masses;
(5) Other acts of inaction, arbitrary action, slow action, or false action that harm the interests of the masses.

Article 127
Where a person, when state property or the lives and property of the masses are under serious threat, is able to rescue but does not, if the circumstances are relatively serious, a Warning, Serious Warning, or Removal from Party Posts shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Probation within the Party or Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 128
Where a person fails to disclose Party affairs, government affairs, factory affairs, village (neighborhood committee) affairs, etc., in accordance with regulations, thereby infringing upon the masses' right to know, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall, if the circumstances are relatively serious, be given a Warning or Serious Warning; if the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts shall be imposed.

Article 129
Where there are other acts in violation of the provisions on the discipline of mass relations, a Warning up to Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed based on the specific circumstances.

Chapter X — Punishment for Acts Violating Work Discipline

Article 130
Where a person is irresponsible in work or negligent in management, failing to effectively implement, inspect, and supervise the implementation of higher-level decisions and deployments, thereby causing significant losses to the interests of the Party, the state, the people, or public property, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall be given a Warning or Serious Warning; where major losses are caused, Removal from Party Posts, Probation within the Party, or Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Where a leading Party cadre passively avoids or shifts responsibility for issues that existed before his or her assumption of office and that fall within the scope of his or her duties, thereby causing serious harm or serious adverse influence, the preceding paragraph shall apply.

Article 131
Where a person dares not to engage in struggle or to take on responsibility in work, and retreats in the face of major conflicts, crises, or difficulties, thereby causing adverse influence or serious consequences, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts, Probation within the Party, or Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 132
Any of the following acts causing serious harm or serious adverse influence, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall be given a Warning or Serious Warning; where the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed:

(1) Being keen on creating a media buzz while remaining superficial;
(2) Simply using meetings to implement meetings and documents to implement documents, without taking concrete action in actual work;
(3) Being detached from reality, failing to conduct in-depth investigation and research, and engaging in arbitrary decision-making or mechanical implementation;
(4) Violating the relevant regulations on streamlining documents and meetings to create a mountain of documents and a sea of meetings;
(5) Engaging in excessive layering of requirements and excessive documentation in supervision, inspection, and assessment work, thereby increasing the burden on grassroots work;
(6) Other acts of formalism and bureaucracy in work.

Article 133
Where a person fails to perform or fails to correctly perform the duties of publicity, education, supervision, and management in the management of official meal services or unit canteen meals, leading to food waste and causing serious adverse influence, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall be given a Warning or Serious Warning; where the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts shall be imposed.

Article 134
Any of the following acts in the work of organizational establishment, causing adverse influence or serious consequences, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall be given a Warning or Serious Warning; where the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed:

(1) Adjusting functions, setting up organizations, determining the number of leadership positions, and deploying personnel, beyond the scope of the "three designations" (designated functions, designated internal bodies, and designated staffing) regulations;
(2) Improperly intervening in the establishment of local organizations;
(3) Other acts in violation of the regulations on organizational establishment management.

Article 135
Any of the following acts in petition work, causing adverse influence or serious consequences, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall be given a Warning or Serious Warning; where the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed:

(1) Failing to accept or handle petition matters in accordance with regulations;
(2) Failing to effectively handle large-scale collective petitions, leading to escalation of the situation;
(3) Paying insufficient attention to the suggestions from Party committee and government petition departments for improving work and refining policies, and failing to implement them effectively, leading to long-unresolved problems;
(4) Other acts of failing to perform or failing to correctly perform the duties of petition work.

Where a person fails to perform or fails to correctly perform duties, leading to the occurrence of petition matters and causing adverse influence or serious consequences, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall be dealt with in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

Article 136
Any of the following acts by a Party organization, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall, if the circumstances are relatively serious, be given a Warning or Serious Warning; if the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed:

(1) During the period when a Party member is under case filing and investigation, authorizing without approval the member's business trip, overseas travel, resignation, or making arrangements for the member's transfer, promotion, rank advancement, further use, reward, or processing of retirement procedures;
(2) After a Party member is subject to criminal liability in accordance with law, failing to impose Party disciplinary punishment in accordance with regulations, or failing to impose Party disciplinary punishment for acts of the Party member that violate national laws and regulations when such punishment should be imposed;
(3) After a decision on Party disciplinary punishment or a decision on appeal review is made, failing to implement in accordance with regulations the matters specified in the decision regarding the disciplined person's Party membership, position, rank, treatment, etc.;
(4) After a Party member receives Party disciplinary punishment, failing to carry out daily education, management, and supervision of the disciplined Party member in accordance with the cadre management authority and organizational relationship.

Article 137
Where a person abuses the power of accountability, or is seriously irresponsible in accountability work, causing adverse influence, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall be given a Warning or Serious Warning; where the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts shall be imposed.

Article 138
Where a person, due to irresponsibility in work, causes the defection of a person under his or her management, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall be given a Warning or Serious Warning; where the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts shall be imposed.

Where a person, due to irresponsibility in work, causes the escape or unauthorized departure of a person under his or her management, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall, if the circumstances are relatively serious, be given a Warning or Serious Warning; if the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts shall be imposed.

Article 139
Where a person engages in statistical fraud, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall, if the circumstances are relatively minor, be given a Warning or Serious Warning; if the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Where a person fails to detect statistical fraud, causing serious consequences, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall be given a Warning or Serious Warning; where the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts, Probation within the Party, or Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 140
Where a person, during higher-level inspections or inspection tours, or when reporting or briefing work to higher-level authorities, fails to report or fails to truthfully report matters that should be reported, causing serious harm or serious adverse influence, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall be given a Warning or Serious Warning; where the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed.

Where a person, during higher-level inspections or inspection tours, or when reporting or briefing work to higher-level authorities, connives at, instigates, implies, or forces subordinates to lie to superiors or report false information, a heavier or aggravated punishment shall be imposed.

Article 141
Where a person, in violation of relevant regulations, intervenes or meddles in market economic activities, committing any of the following acts, if the circumstances are relatively minor, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed:

(1) Intervening or meddling in activities such as construction project contracting, land use rights transfer, government procurement, real estate development and operation, mineral resource development and utilization, and intermediary service activities;
(2) Intervening or meddling in matters such as the restructuring, merger, bankruptcy, property rights transaction, asset verification, asset valuation, asset transfer, major project investment, and other major business activities of state-owned enterprises;
(3) Intervening or meddling in the approval of various administrative permits, fund lending, and other matters;
(4) Intervening or meddling in economic disputes;
(5) Intervening or meddling in the use, distribution, contracting, leasing, and other matters concerning collective funds, assets, and resources.

Article 142
Where a person, in violation of relevant regulations, intervenes or meddles in judicial activities, or in disciplinary or law enforcement activities, making inquiries about case details, intervening, or interceding with relevant localities or departments, or otherwise exerting influence over judicial, disciplinary, or law enforcement activities, if the circumstances are relatively minor, a Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Where a person, in violation of relevant regulations, intervenes or meddles in activities such as the allocation of public financial funds, project evaluation and review, or commendation and award of honors and merits, causing major losses or adverse influence, the preceding paragraph shall apply.

Article 143
Where a person who has been entrusted with a request and has the obligation to report and register such intervention or meddling in accordance with relevant regulations fails to report or register as required, if the circumstances are relatively serious, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts shall be imposed.

Article 144
Where a person discloses, disseminates, or inquires about or steals matters concerning the selection and appointment of cadres, disciplinary review, inspection or disciplinary inspection tour, etc., that have not yet been made public by the Party organization, or other content that should be kept confidential, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; where the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Where a person privately retains materials relating to the Party organization's selection and appointment of cadres, disciplinary review, inspection or disciplinary inspection tour, etc., if the circumstances are relatively serious, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts shall be imposed.

Article 145
Where, in examination or admission work, a person leaks examination questions, engages in examination fraud, alters examination papers, or makes irregular admissions in violation of relevant regulations, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; where the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 146
Where a person seeks, through improper means, to have himself or herself or others travel overseas at public expense, if the circumstances are relatively minor, a Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, a Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts shall be imposed.

Article 147
Where a Party member in a temporary overseas delegation (group) or among its personnel, without authorization, extends the period abroad or changes the itinerary, the persons directly responsible and the leading responsible persons shall be given a Warning or Serious Warning; where the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts shall be imposed.

Article 148
Where a Party member in a diplomatic mission abroad or a temporary overseas delegation (group) violates the laws or regulations of the host country or region, or fails to respect the religious customs of the host country or region, if the circumstances are relatively serious, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts, Probation within the Party, or Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 149
Where, in the Party's disciplinary inspection, organizational, propaganda, united front work, or in other work of organs, a person fails to perform or fails to correctly perform duties, causing losses or adverse influence, a Warning up to Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed based on the specific circumstances.

Chapter XI — Punishment for Acts Violating the Discipline of Conduct

Article 150
Where a person leads a life of extravagance, waste, and indulgence in pleasure, and pursues vulgar tastes, thereby causing adverse influence, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts shall be imposed.

Article 151
Where a person engages in improper sexual relations with others, thereby causing adverse influence, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; where the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Where a person, using his or her authority, or a relationship of upbringing, dependency, or other similar relationship, engages in sexual relations with another person, a heavier punishment shall be imposed.

Article 152
Where a leading Party cadre does not attach importance to the cultivation of family values, and fails to properly supervise and educate his or her spouse, children, or their spouses, causing adverse influence or serious consequences, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Removal from Party Posts shall be imposed.

Article 153
Where a person violates public order and good customs, and engages in improper statements or conduct in public places or in cyberspace, causing adverse influence, a Warning or Serious Warning shall be imposed; where the circumstances are relatively serious, Removal from Party Posts or Probation within the Party shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed.

Article 154
Where there are other acts of serious violation of social morality or family virtues, a Warning up to Expulsion from the Party shall be imposed based on the specific circumstances.

Part III — Supplementary Provisions

Article 155
The Party committees of the provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in accordance with this Regulation and in light of the actual circumstances of their respective work, formulate individual implementing provisions.

Article 156
The Central Military Commission may, in accordance with this Regulation and in light of the actual circumstances of the Chinese People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force, formulate supplementary provisions or individual provisions.

Article 157
This Regulation shall be interpreted by the Central Commission for Discipline Inspection.

Article 158
This Regulation shall come into force on January 1, 2024.
```
