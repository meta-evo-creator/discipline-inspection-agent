# Discipline Inspection Methodology — Dual-Factor Analysis Framework (Violation + Culpability)

> A methodology reference for discipline inspection officials, not a technical document for agents.

---

## Core Formula

> **Disciplinary Violation = Violation Elements (objective) + Culpability Elements (subjective) + No Exculpatory Circumstances**

Source: CCDI Case Review Office — *Introduction and Connotation of "Disciplinary Reasoning"*

---

## Step 1: Violation Determination (6 Checks)

Check each item. All satisfied → violation established.

| # | Element | Why It Matters |
|:-:|:-----|:-----|
| 1 | **Subject** | Not everyone is subject to the same regulation — Party members are governed by the Disciplinary Punishment Regulations; non-Party members by the Administrative Discipline Law or Public Institution Personnel Regulations |
| 2 | **Prohibited Conduct** | What specifically was done? Which article was violated? Must match the original text — cannot rely on intuition |
| 3 | **Protected Interest** | What legal interest was harmed? (integrity, fairness of official duties, organizational discipline, etc.) |
| 4 | **Harmful Consequence** | What consequences or adverse effects resulted? |
| 5 | **Causation** | Is there an objective causal link between the conduct and the consequence? |
| 6 | **Justification Defense** | Is there a legitimate reason? (necessity, lawful official act, superior order) |

## Step 2: Culpability Determination (5 Checks)

| # | Element | Key Question |
|:-:|:-----|:-----|
| 1 | **Intent** | Did they know it was wrong but did it anyway? Did they desire or knowingly allow the result? |
| 2 | **Negligence** | Should they have foreseen it but didn't? Did they carelessly assume it could be avoided? |
| 3 | **Capacity** | Do they have the cognitive/disciplinary capacity? (age, mental state, training records) |
| 4 | **Motive/Purpose** | Why did they do it? For personal gain or under duress? |
| 5 | **Exculpatory Defense** | Was there force majeure / coercion / institutional compulsion? |

## 6-Dimension Scoring (24-Character Guideline)

| Dimension | Weight | Check Content |
|:-----|:--:|:-----|
| Accurate Characterization | 25% | Complete article citations? Original text comparison? Three-element verification? |
| Clear Facts | 20% | Complete conduct chain? Time/location/amount chain clear? |
| Solid Evidence | 20% | Complete evidence inventory? Indirect evidence chain viable? |
| Appropriate Disposition | 15% | Sanction level matches regulations and facts? |
| Complete Procedures | 10% | Interview procedures compliant? Rights/obligations disclosed? |
| Procedural Compliance | 10% | Investigation strategy lawful? Evidence collection path legal? |

≥80 PASS | 60-79 REVISE | <60 REJECT

## Enhancement Tools (Optional)

The two-step base framework covers 90% of routine cases. The following tools are for complex cases (systemic violations, repeat offenses, institutional deficiencies):

| Tool | Use Case | One-Line Operation |
|:-----|:-----|:-----|
| **M6 Swiss Cheese Audit** | Systemic, recurring problems | Trace five defense layers: operational → managerial → institutional → supervisory → cultural |
| **M7 Attribution Calibration** | Preventing labeling bias | Self-check: Would a reasonable person in the same position have done the same thing? |
| **M8 Just Culture** | Final confirmation before sanction | Classify into three types: reckless / at-risk behavior / human error |
| **M9 Scapegoat Audit** | Preventing "punish the person, ignore the system" | Self-check: If we only punish the person without fixing the system, will this recur within three months? |

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It's Wrong | Correct Approach |
|:-----|:-----|:-----|
| Citing articles from memory | Article numbers and versions may be misremembered | Use ripgrep to verify original text in WIKI + annotate version |
| Only looking at adverse provisions | Only listing provisions supporting sanctions, missing mitigating factors | Article 17 mitigating/aggravating provisions must be checked simultaneously |
| Premature sanction judgment | Determining sanction level before facts are established | "Investigation → Assessment" two-step method — facts are facts, law is law |
| Ignoring identity prerequisites | Citing Disciplinary Punishment Regulations without confirming Party membership | "Conditional applicability" annotation — Party discipline provisions apply only after Party membership is confirmed |
