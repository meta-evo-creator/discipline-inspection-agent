# Supervision Law of the People's Republic of China (2024 Amendment)

## Content

**Supervision Law of the People's Republic of China**

(Adopted at the First Session of the Thirteenth National People's Congress on March 20, 2018; amended in accordance with the Decision on Amending the Supervision Law of the People's Republic of China adopted at the Thirteenth Session of the Standing Committee of the Fourteenth National People's Congress on December 25, 2024)

## Chapter I: General Provisions

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution for the purposes of deepening the work on integrity-building and anti-corruption, strengthening oversight over all public officials who exercise public power, achieving full coverage of state supervision, continuously deepening the reform of the state supervision system, and advancing the modernization of the national governance system and governance capacity.

**Article 2** The leadership of the Communist Party of China over state supervision work shall be upheld, with Marxism-Leninism, Mao Zedong Thought, Deng Xiaoping Theory, the important thought of Three Represents, the Scientific Outlook on Development, and Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era as guidance, to establish a centralized, unified, authoritative, and efficient state supervision system with Chinese characteristics.

**Article 3** Supervision commissions at all levels are the specialized organs exercising the functions of state supervision. In accordance with this Law, they shall supervise all public officials who exercise public power (hereinafter referred to as "public officials"), investigate duty-related violations and duty-related crimes, carry out integrity-building and anti-corruption work, and uphold the dignity of the Constitution and laws.

**Article 4** Supervision commissions shall independently exercise the power of supervision in accordance with legal provisions and shall not be subject to interference by administrative organs, social organizations, or individuals.

When handling cases of duty-related violations and duty-related crimes, supervisory organs shall cooperate with and mutually constrain trial organs, procuratorial organs, and law enforcement departments.

When supervisory organs require assistance in their work, relevant organs and units shall provide assistance in accordance with the law as required by the supervisory organs.

**Article 5** State supervision work shall strictly follow the Constitution and laws, be based on facts and take law as the criterion; match powers with responsibilities and exercise strict supervision; follow statutory procedures and perform duties impartially; respect and safeguard human rights, apply the law equally to all, and protect the lawful rights and interests of supervisees and relevant persons; and combine punishment with education, applying leniency and severity appropriately.

**Article 6** State supervision work shall adhere to treating both symptoms and root causes and comprehensive governance, strengthen supervision and accountability, and severely punish corruption; deepen reform and improve the rule of law to effectively constrain and supervise power; strengthen legal education and moral education, promote excellent traditional Chinese culture, and establish a long-term mechanism ensuring that officials dare not, cannot, and do not want to engage in corruption.

## Chapter II: Supervisory Organs and Their Functions and Duties

**Article 7** The National Commission of Supervision of the People's Republic of China is the highest supervisory organ.

Supervision commissions shall be established in provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, autonomous prefectures, counties, autonomous counties, cities not divided into districts, and municipal districts.

**Article 8** The National Commission of Supervision is created by the National People's Congress and is responsible for national supervision work.

The National Commission of Supervision is composed of one Chairperson, a number of Vice-Chairpersons, and a number of Members. The Chairperson is elected by the National People's Congress, and the Vice-Chairpersons and Members are appointed or removed by the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress upon the recommendation of the Chairperson of the National Commission of Supervision.

The term of office of the Chairperson of the National Commission of Supervision shall be the same as the term of office of the National People's Congress, and the Chairperson shall serve no more than two consecutive terms.

The National Commission of Supervision shall be responsible to the National People's Congress and its Standing Committee and shall accept their oversight.

**Article 9** Local supervision commissions at all levels are created by the people's congresses at the corresponding level and are responsible for supervision work within their respective administrative regions.

Each local supervision commission is composed of one Chairperson, a number of Vice-Chairpersons, and a number of Members. The Chairperson is elected by the people's congress at the corresponding level, and the Vice-Chairpersons and Members are appointed or removed by the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level upon the recommendation of the Chairperson of the supervision commission.

The term of office of the Chairperson of a local supervision commission shall be the same as the term of office of the people's congress at the corresponding level.

Local supervision commissions shall be responsible to the people's congress at the corresponding level and its standing committee and to the supervision commission at the next higher level, and shall accept their oversight.

**Article 10** The National Commission of Supervision shall lead the work of local supervision commissions at all levels, and a supervision commission at a higher level shall lead the work of supervision commissions at lower levels.

**Article 11** A supervision commission shall, in accordance with this Law and relevant legal provisions, perform the functions and duties of supervision, investigation, and disposition:

(1) Carrying out integrity education for public officials, and supervising and inspecting their performance of duties in accordance with the law, their exercise of power impartially, their integrity in governance and professional conduct, and their moral conduct;

(2) Investigating duty-related violations and duty-related crimes suspected of bribery and corruption, abuse of power, dereliction of duty, rent-seeking, transfer of interests, favoritism and fraud, and waste of state assets;

(3) Making decisions on administrative sanctions against public officials who have violated the law in accordance with the law; holding accountable leading persons who have failed to perform their duties effectively or have been derelict in their responsibilities; transferring investigation results to the people's procuratorate for review and initiation of public prosecution where duty-related crimes are suspected; and making supervision recommendations to the unit to which the supervisee belongs.

**Article 12** Supervision commissions at all levels may station or dispatch supervision agencies or supervision commissioners to organs of the Communist Party of China at the corresponding level, state organs, organs of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference, organizations and units authorized or entrusted by laws and regulations to manage public affairs, as well as specific areas within their jurisdiction, state-owned enterprises, public institutions, and other units.

With the approval of the National Commission of Supervision, supervision agencies and supervision commissioners stationed by the National Commission of Supervision in units under vertical management or dual leadership with primary leadership from the higher-level unit, and in state-owned enterprises, may further dispatch personnel to units at the next lower level of the host unit.

With the approval of the National Commission of Supervision, supervision agencies and supervision commissioners stationed by the National Commission of Supervision may further dispatch personnel to regular institutions of higher education whose leadership is managed by the host unit; supervision agencies stationed by the National Commission of Supervision in the State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission of the State Council may further dispatch personnel to state-owned enterprises whose leadership is managed by the host unit.

Supervision agencies and supervision commissioners shall be responsible to the supervision commission or the supervision agency or supervision commissioner that stationed or dispatched them.

**Article 13** Stationed or dispatched supervision agencies and supervision commissioners shall, as authorized, supervise public officials in accordance with their management authority, make supervision recommendations, and investigate and dispose of matters involving public officials in accordance with the law.

**Article 14** The state shall implement a supervision officer system, establishing in accordance with the law the systems for determining the ranks, appointment and removal, evaluation, and promotion of supervision officers.

## Chapter III: Scope of Supervision and Jurisdiction

**Article 15** Supervisory organs shall supervise the following public officials and relevant personnel:

(1) Civil servants in organs of the Communist Party of China, organs of the People's Congress and its Standing Committee, people's governments, supervision commissions, people's courts, people's procuratorates, organs of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference at all levels, democratic party organs, and federations of industry and commerce, as well as personnel managed by reference to the Civil Service Law of the People's Republic of China;

(2) Persons engaged in public service in organizations authorized by laws or regulations or entrusted by state organs in accordance with the law to manage public affairs;

(3) Management personnel in state-owned enterprises;

(4) Personnel engaged in management in publicly-funded units of education, scientific research, culture, medical and health care, sports, and others;

(5) Personnel engaged in management in grassroots self-governing mass organizations;

(6) Other persons who perform public duties in accordance with the law.

**Article 16** Supervision commissions at all levels shall, in accordance with their management authority, have jurisdiction over supervision matters involving the persons specified in Article 15 of this Law within their respective administrative regions.

A supervision commission at a higher level may handle supervision matters within the jurisdiction of a supervision commission at the next lower level and may, when necessary, also handle supervision matters within the jurisdiction of supervision commissions at various levels under its authority.

Where there is a dispute over jurisdiction over a supervision matter between supervision commissions, the common supervision commission at the next higher level shall determine the jurisdiction.

**Article 17** A supervision commission at a higher level may designate a supervision matter under its own jurisdiction to a supervision commission at a lower level for handling, and may also designate a supervision matter within the jurisdiction of a lower-level supervision commission to another supervision commission for handling.

Where a supervision commission considers a supervision matter under its jurisdiction to be significant or complex and requiring the jurisdiction of the supervision commission at the next higher level, it may request the supervision commission at the next higher level to assume jurisdiction.

## Chapter IV: Supervisory Powers and Authority

**Article 18** When exercising the functions and duties of supervision and investigation, supervisory organs shall have the authority to gather information and collect and obtain evidence from relevant units and individuals in accordance with the law. The relevant units and individuals shall provide truthful information.

Supervisory organs and their staff shall keep confidential any state secrets, work secrets, trade secrets, personal privacy, and personal information that come to their knowledge during the process of supervision and investigation.

No unit or individual may forge, conceal, or destroy evidence.

**Article 19** Where a supervisee may have committed a duty-related violation, the supervisory organ may, in accordance with its management authority, conduct a direct interview or entrust a relevant organ or person to conduct an interview, or send a letter of inquiry, requesting an explanation of the circumstances.

**Article 20** During the investigation process, the supervisory organ may interview a person under investigation suspected of a duty-related violation, requesting that person to make a statement regarding the suspected illegal conduct, and when necessary, issue a written notice to the person under investigation.

The supervisory organ may interrogate a person under investigation suspected of duty-related crimes such as bribery and corruption, dereliction of duty, and malfeasance, requesting that person to truthfully confess the circumstances of the suspected crime.

**Article 21** The supervisory organ may, upon approval in accordance with the law based on the circumstances of the case, compel a person under investigation suspected of serious duty-related violations or duty-related crimes to appear for investigation.

**Article 22** During the investigation process, the supervisory organ may question witnesses and other persons.

**Article 23** Where a person under investigation is suspected of serious duty-related violations or duty-related crimes and falls under any of the following circumstances, the supervisory organ may, upon approval in accordance with the law, take the measure of ordered placement under surveillance:

(1) The circumstances set forth in paragraph 1 of Article 24 of this Law do not exist;

(2) The conditions for custodial detention are met, but the person suffers from a serious illness and cannot take care of themselves, is a pregnant woman or a woman nursing her own infant, or is the sole caregiver of a person who cannot take care of themselves;

(3) The case has not yet been concluded, but the custodial detention period has expired, or it is no longer necessary to continue custodial detention of the person;

(4) The conditions for custodial detention are met, but given the special circumstances of the case or the needs of case handling, the measure of ordered placement under surveillance is more appropriate.

A person subject to ordered placement under surveillance shall comply with the following provisions:

(1) Not leave, without the approval of the supervisory organ, the urban districts of the municipality directly under the Central Government or the city divided into districts where they reside, or the jurisdiction of the county or city not divided into districts;

(2) Report to the supervisory organ within 24 hours any change in their address, work unit, or contact information;

(3) Present themselves for investigation in a timely manner upon receiving notice;

(4) Not interfere with the testimony of witnesses in any form;

(5) Not collude in the making of confessions or forge, conceal, or destroy evidence.

Where a person subject to ordered placement under surveillance violates the provisions of the preceding paragraph, and the circumstances are serious, custodial detention may be imposed in accordance with the law.

**Article 24** Where a person under investigation is suspected of serious duty-related violations or duty-related crimes such as bribery and corruption, dereliction of duty, and malfeasance, the supervisory organ has already obtained part of the facts and evidence of the illegal or criminal conduct, but significant issues remain requiring further investigation, and falls under any of the following circumstances, the supervisory organ may, upon approval in accordance with the law, detain the person in a designated place:

(1) The case involves significant or complex circumstances;

(2) The person may attempt to escape or commit suicide;

(3) The person may collude in the making of confessions or forge, conceal, or destroy evidence;

(4) There may be other conduct obstructing the investigation.

With respect to persons involved in the case who are suspected of the crime of offering bribes or joint duty-related crimes, the supervisory organ may take custodial detention measures in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

The establishment, management, and oversight of custodial detention facilities shall be carried out in accordance with relevant state provisions.

**Article 25** With respect to the following persons who have not been placed under custodial detention, where the supervisory organ discovers that there is a significant safety risk such as flight or suicide, it may, upon approval in accordance with the law, place them under protective custody:

(1) Persons who voluntarily surrender and are suspected of serious duty-related violations or duty-related crimes;

(2) Persons who, during interviews, correspondence inquiries, or questioning, confess to being suspected of serious duty-related violations or duty-related crimes;

(3) Persons who, during interrogation, voluntarily confess to being suspected of major duty-related crimes.

After protective custody measures are taken, the person under protective custody shall be immediately transferred to a custodial detention facility, no later than 24 hours.

**Article 26** Where a supervisory organ investigates serious duty-related violations or duty-related crimes such as bribery and corruption, dereliction of duty, and malfeasance, it may, as required by the work and in accordance with regulations, inquire into and freeze the deposits, remittances, bonds, stocks, fund shares, and other property of the units and individuals involved. The relevant units and individuals shall cooperate.

Where frozen property is determined after investigation to be unrelated to the case, the freeze shall be lifted and the property returned within three days after such determination.

**Article 27** A supervisory organ may search the person, articles, residence, and other relevant locations of a person under investigation suspected of a duty-related crime, as well as persons who may be hiding the person under investigation or criminal evidence. When conducting a search, a search warrant shall be presented, and the person under investigation or their family members and other witnesses shall be present.

A search of the body of a female shall be conducted by female personnel.

When conducting a search, the supervisory organ may request the assistance of a public security organ as required by the work. The public security organ shall provide assistance in accordance with the law.

**Article 28** During the investigation process, a supervisory organ may obtain, seal, or seize property, documents, electronic data, and other information used to prove that the person under investigation is suspected of illegal or criminal conduct. When taking measures to obtain, seal, or seize evidence, original items and original documents shall be collected. In the presence of the holder or custodian and witnesses, the items shall be photographed, registered, and numbered one by one, and an inventory shall be prepared. The persons present shall check and sign the inventory on the spot, and a copy of the inventory shall be provided to the holder or custodian of the property or documents.

For property and documents obtained, sealed, or seized, the supervisory organ shall establish a dedicated account, dedicated premises, and designate dedicated personnel for proper custody, strictly handle the procedures for transfer and retrieval, conduct regular reconciliation and verification, and shall not damage or use them for other purposes. Items of unknown value shall be appraised in a timely manner and stored separately under seal.

Where property or documents that have been sealed or seized are determined after investigation to be unrelated to the case, the seal or seizure shall be lifted and the property or documents shall be returned within three days after such determination.

**Article 29** During the investigation process, the supervisory organ may, directly or by assigning or engaging persons with specialized knowledge and under the direction of investigators, conduct inspections and examinations. A record of the inspections and examinations shall be made, signed or sealed by the persons participating in the inspections and examinations and the witnesses.

When necessary, the supervisory organ may conduct investigative experiments. A record of the investigative experiments shall be made, signed or sealed by the persons participating in the experiments.

**Article 30** During the investigation process, for specialized issues in a case, the supervisory organ may assign or engage persons with specialized knowledge to conduct expert evaluations. After conducting an expert evaluation, the expert shall produce an expert evaluation opinion and sign it.

**Article 31** Where a supervisory organ investigates suspected major duty-related crimes such as bribery and corruption, it may, as required and after undergoing strict approval procedures, take technical investigation measures and, in accordance with regulations, assign relevant organs to execute them.

The approval decision shall specify the type of technical investigation measures and the targets to whom they apply, and shall be valid for up to three months from the date of issuance. For complex or difficult cases where it is necessary to continue taking technical investigation measures after the period has expired, the validity period may be extended upon approval, with each extension not exceeding three months. Where it is no longer necessary to continue taking technical investigation measures, they shall be promptly lifted.

**Article 32** If a person under investigation who should be subject to custodial detention is at large, the supervisory organ may decide to issue a wanted notice within its administrative region, and the public security organ shall issue the wanted order to pursue and capture the person. If the wanted scope extends beyond the administrative region, the matter shall be reported to the superior supervisory organ with the authority to decide.

**Article 33** To prevent the person under investigation and relevant personnel from fleeing abroad, the supervisory organ may, upon approval by a supervisory organ at or above the provincial level, take measures to restrict the exit of the person under investigation and relevant personnel, to be executed by the public security organ in accordance with the law. Where it is no longer necessary to continue such exit restriction measures, they shall be promptly lifted.

**Article 34** Where a person under investigation suspected of a duty-related crime voluntarily admits guilt and accepts punishment, and falls under any of the following circumstances, the supervisory organ may, after collective study by the leading personnel and reporting to the supervisory organ at the next higher level for approval, propose a recommendation for lenient punishment when transferring the case to the people's procuratorate:

(1) Voluntarily surrendering and sincerely repenting;

(2) Actively cooperating with the investigation and truthfully confessing illegal or criminal conduct not yet known to the supervisory organ;

(3) Actively returning illegally obtained gains to reduce losses;

(4) Performing significant meritorious service or the case involves major national interests, among other circumstances.

**Article 35** Where a person involved in a duty-related violation or duty-related crime exposes the duty-related violation or criminal conduct of a person under investigation, and the exposure is verified, or provides important clues that assist in the investigation of other cases, the supervisory organ may, after collective study by the leading personnel and reporting to the supervisory organ at the next higher level for approval, propose a recommendation for lenient punishment when transferring the case to the people's procuratorate.

**Article 36** Physical evidence, documentary evidence, witness testimony, statements and exculpations of the person under investigation, audio-visual materials, electronic data, and other evidentiary materials collected by the supervisory organ in accordance with the provisions of this Law may be used as evidence in criminal proceedings.

When collecting, preserving, reviewing, and using evidence, the supervisory organ shall be consistent with the requirements and standards for evidence in criminal trials.

Evidence collected by illegal methods shall be excluded in accordance with the law and shall not be used as a basis for case disposition.

**Article 37** Where a people's court, people's procuratorate, public security organ, auditing organ, or other state organ discovers during its work leads concerning a public official suspected of duty-related violations such as bribery and corruption, dereliction of duty, or duty-related crimes, it shall transfer such leads to the supervisory organ, which shall investigate and handle them in accordance with the law.

Where a person under investigation is suspected of both serious duty-related violations or duty-related crimes and other illegal or criminal conduct, the supervisory organ shall generally take the lead in the investigation, with other organs providing assistance.

## Chapter V: Supervisory Procedures

**Article 38** Supervisory organs shall accept reports or tip-offs and handle them in accordance with relevant regulations. Where a matter is not within the organ's jurisdiction, it shall be transferred to the competent organ for handling.

**Article 39** Supervisory organs shall conduct their work strictly in accordance with procedures and establish a working mechanism of coordination and mutual constraint among the departments handling lead disposition, investigation, and adjudication.

Supervisory organs shall strengthen the supervision and management of the entire process of investigation and disposition work, establish corresponding work departments to perform management and coordination functions such as lead management, supervision and inspection, supervision of handling, and statistical analysis.

**Article 40** With respect to leads concerning supervisees, supervisory organs shall, in accordance with relevant regulations, propose disposition opinions, go through approval procedures, and handle them by category. The disposition of leads shall be regularly summarized, reported, periodically inspected, and spot-checked.

**Article 41** Where a preliminary verification method is needed to dispose of a lead, the supervisory organ shall undergo approval procedures in accordance with the law and establish a verification team. After the preliminary verification work is completed, the verification team shall draft a preliminary verification report and propose handling recommendations. The responsible department shall propose classified handling opinions. The preliminary verification report and classified handling opinions shall be submitted to the principal person of the supervisory organ for approval.

**Article 42** After preliminary verification, where a supervisee is suspected of duty-related violation or crime and legal liability needs to be pursued, the supervisory organ shall go through the case-filing procedures in accordance with the prescribed authority and procedures.

After the principal person of the supervisory organ approves the case filing in accordance with the law, a special meeting shall be held to study and determine the investigation plan and decide on the investigation measures to be taken.

The decision to file a case for investigation shall be announced to the person under investigation and reported to the relevant organization. Where serious duty-related violations or duty-related crimes are suspected, the family members of the person under investigation shall be notified, and the matter shall be publicly announced.

**Article 43** Supervisory organs shall investigate cases of duty-related violations and duty-related crimes, collect evidence regarding whether the person under investigation has engaged in illegal or criminal conduct and the severity of such conduct, ascertain the facts of the illegal or criminal conduct, and form a complete and stable chain of evidence that cross-confirms each other.

Investigators shall conduct investigation work in a lawful, civilized, and standardized manner. It is strictly prohibited to collect evidence by violent, threatening, inducing, deceitful, or other illegal means. It is strictly prohibited to insult, physically assault, abuse, physically punish, or covertly physically punish the person under investigation or persons involved in the case.

Supervisory organs and their staff shall, in the course of performing their duties, protect the property rights and independent management rights of enterprises in accordance with the law, and shall strictly prohibit the illegal interference with enterprise production and operations through the abuse of power. Where the assistance of enterprise operators in investigations is required, their personal rights, property rights, and other lawful rights and interests shall be protected, and the impact on the normal production and operation activities of the enterprise shall be avoided or minimized.

**Article 44** Investigators taking investigation measures such as interrogation, questioning, compulsory appearance, ordered placement under surveillance, protective custody, custodial detention, search, obtaining evidence, sealing, seizure, inspection and examination shall, in accordance with regulations, present their credentials and produce written notices. Such measures shall be carried out by no fewer than two persons, and written materials such as records and reports shall be produced and signed or sealed by the relevant persons.

When conducting interrogation and important evidence collection work such as search, sealing, and seizure, investigators shall make audio and video recordings of the entire process for retention and future reference.

**Article 45** Investigators shall strictly implement the investigation plan and shall not arbitrarily expand the scope of investigation or change the targets or matters of investigation.

Important matters during the investigation process shall be reported and requested for instructions following collective study in accordance with procedures.

**Article 46** The measures of compulsory appearance, ordered placement under surveillance, or protective custody shall be taken in accordance with the prescribed authority and procedures, upon approval by the principal person of the supervisory organ.

The duration of compulsory appearance shall not exceed twelve hours. Where measures of protective custody or custodial detention need to be taken, the duration of compulsory appearance shall not exceed twenty-four hours. A person under investigation shall not be subjected to disguised detention by means of continuous compulsory appearance.

Ordered placement under surveillance shall not exceed twelve months at most.

Where the supervisory organ takes protective custody measures, it shall make a decision on custodial detention or release from protective custody within seven days, provided that in special circumstances the period may be extended by one to three days.

**Article 47** The decision to take custodial detention measures by a supervisory organ shall be made after collective study by the leading personnel of the supervisory organ. Where a supervision commission at or below the level of a city divided into districts takes custodial detention measures, it shall report to the supervisory organ at the next higher level for approval. Where a provincial-level supervisory organ takes custodial detention measures, it shall report to the National Commission of Supervision for recordation.

**Article 48** The duration of custodial detention shall not exceed three months. In special circumstances, it may be extended once, and the extension shall not exceed three months. Where a supervisory organ at or below the provincial level takes custodial detention measures, an extension of the custodial detention period shall be reported to the supervisory organ at the next higher level for approval. Where a supervisory organ finds that custodial detention measures were improperly taken or that it is no longer necessary to continue such measures, the measures shall be promptly lifted or changed to the measure of ordered placement under surveillance.

Where a person under investigation suspected of a duty-related crime may be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of ten years or more, and the investigation still cannot be concluded upon the expiration of the extension period in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the period may be further extended by two months with the approval or decision of the National Commission of Supervision.

Where, during the investigation, a provincial-level or higher supervisory organ discovers that the person under investigation suspected of a duty-related crime is also suspected of a major duty-related crime of a different type from the crime for which custodial detention was imposed, or a major duty-related crime of the same type that affects the determination of the charge or the sentencing grade, the period of custodial detention shall be recalculated from the date of such discovery, with the approval or decision of the National Commission of Supervision. The recalculation of the custodial detention period shall be limited to one occasion.

**Article 49** Where a supervisory organ takes measures of compulsory appearance, ordered placement under surveillance, protective custody, or custodial detention, it may, as required by the work, request the assistance of a public security organ. The public security organ shall provide assistance in accordance with the law.

Guard duties at custodial detention facilities of supervisory organs at or below the provincial level shall be the responsibility of public security organs. Guard duties at custodial detention facilities of the National Commission of Supervision shall be separately provided for by the state. The management of custodial detention guard teams shall be carried out in accordance with relevant state provisions.

**Article 50** Within 24 hours after the measures of protective custody or custodial detention are taken, the unit to which the person under protective custody or custodial detention belongs and their family members shall be notified, except where there are circumstances that may impede the investigation, such as the possibility of forging, concealing, or destroying evidence, interfering with witness testimony, or colluding in confessions. When the circumstances impeding the investigation have disappeared, the unit to which the person under protective custody or custodial detention belongs and their family members shall be notified immediately. Upon the release of protective custody or custodial detention, the unit to which the person belongs and their family members shall be notified in a timely manner.

A person under protective custody or custodial detention and their close relatives have the right to apply for a change of the protective custody or custodial detention measures. After receiving the application, the supervisory organ shall make a decision within three days. Where the change of measures is not approved, the applicant shall be informed and the reasons for the refusal shall be explained.

Supervisory organs shall ensure the food, rest, safety, and medical services of persons subject to compulsory appearance, protective custody, and custodial detention. When conducting interviews or interrogations, the time and duration shall be reasonably arranged. Records of interviews and interrogations shall be signed by the interviewee or the person interrogated after review.

Where a person under protective custody or custodial detention is transferred to a judicial organ on suspicion of a crime and is subsequently sentenced to public surveillance, criminal detention, or fixed-term imprisonment, one day of protective custody or custodial detention shall be offset against two days of public surveillance, or against one day of criminal detention or fixed-term imprisonment.

**Article 51** After the investigation work is concluded, the supervisory organ shall, in accordance with the law, conduct a comprehensive adjudication of the case facts and evidence, determination of the nature, procedures and formalities, and property involved in the case, produce an adjudication report, and submit it for collective deliberation.

**Article 52** Based on the results of supervision and investigation, the supervisory organ shall make the following dispositions in accordance with the law:

(1) For public officials who have committed duty-related violations but with minor circumstances, directly or through entrusting a relevant organ or person, conduct a talk as a reminder, criticize and educate, order self-examination, or issue a letter of admonition, in accordance with management authority;

(2) Make decisions on administrative sanctions such as warning, demerit recording, major demerit recording, demotion, removal from office, and expulsion against public officials who have violated the law, in accordance with statutory procedures;

(3) Make a direct decision on accountability against leading persons responsible for failing to perform or correctly perform their duties, in accordance with management authority, or propose accountability recommendations to the organ with the authority to make accountability decisions;

(4) Where a duty-related crime is suspected and the supervisory organ, after investigation, considers the facts of the crime to be clear and the evidence to be reliable and sufficient, prepare a prosecution opinion and transfer it together with the case files and evidence to the people's procuratorate for examination and initiation of public prosecution;

(5) Propose supervision recommendations on issues existing in the integrity-building and performance of duties of the unit to which the supervisee belongs.

Where, after investigation, there is no evidence proving that the person under investigation has engaged in illegal or criminal conduct, the supervisory organ shall dismiss the case and notify the unit to which the person under investigation belongs.

**Article 53** With respect to property obtained through illegal means and as determined after investigation, the supervisory organ shall confiscate, recover, or order restitution or compensation in accordance with the law. With respect to property obtained through suspected criminal conduct, the supervisory organ shall transfer it together with the case to the people's procuratorate.

**Article 54** With respect to cases transferred by a supervisory organ, the people's procuratorate shall take compulsory measures against the person under investigation in accordance with the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

Where, after examination, the people's procuratorate considers that the facts of the crime have been clearly ascertained, the evidence is reliable and sufficient, and criminal liability should be pursued in accordance with the law, it shall make a decision to prosecute.

Where, after examination, the people's procuratorate considers that supplementary verification is needed, it shall return the case to the supervisory organ for supplementary investigation and may, when necessary, conduct supplementary investigation on its own. Supplementary investigation of a case shall be completed within one month. Supplementary investigation shall be limited to two occasions.

Where circumstances for not prosecuting exist as provided in the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the people's procuratorate shall, upon approval by the people's procuratorate at the next higher level, make a decision of non-prosecution in accordance with the law. Where a supervisory organ considers a decision of non-prosecution to be erroneous, it may request reconsideration from the people's procuratorate at the next higher level.

**Article 55** Where, during the investigation of duty-related crimes such as bribery and corruption, dereliction of duty, and malfeasance, the person under investigation absconds or dies and it is necessary to continue the investigation, the investigation shall be continued and a conclusion shall be reached. Where the person under investigation has absconded and cannot be brought to justice after one year of pursuit, or has died, the supervisory organ shall request the people's procuratorate to apply to the people's court, in accordance with statutory procedures, for the confiscation of illegal gains.

**Article 56** Where a supervisee disagrees with a disposition decision made by the supervisory organ concerning themselves, they may, within one month from the date of receipt of the disposition decision, apply for review to the supervisory organ that made the decision. The reviewing organ shall make a review decision within one month. Where the supervisee still disagrees with the review decision, they may, within one month from the date of receipt of the review decision, apply for a reexamination to the supervisory organ at the next higher level. The reexamining organ shall make a reexamination decision within two months. During the period of review and reexamination, execution of the original disposition decision shall not be suspended. Where, after review, the reexamining organ determines that the disposition decision is erroneous, the original decision-making organ shall promptly correct it.

## Chapter VI: International Cooperation Against Corruption

**Article 57** The National Commission of Supervision shall coordinate international exchanges and cooperation with other countries, regions, and international organizations in combating corruption, and organize the implementation of international anti-corruption treaties.

**Article 58** The National Commission of Supervision shall work together with relevant units to strengthen cooperation and judicial assistance with relevant countries, regions, and international organizations in areas of extradition, transfer of sentenced persons, repatriation, joint investigations, evidence collection, asset recovery, and information exchange in the fight against corruption.

**Article 59** The National Commission of Supervision shall strengthen organizational coordination in international fugitive pursuit, asset recovery, and prevention of flight, and urge relevant units to carry out the following related work:

(1) For major duty-related crime cases such as bribery and corruption, dereliction of duty, and malfeasance, where the person under investigation has fled abroad and the evidence is relatively conclusive, pursue and capture the person through international cooperation in overseas fugitive pursuit;

(2) Request the country where the illicit money and property are located to inquire into, freeze, seize, confiscate, recover, and return the assets involved;

(3) Inquire into and monitor the entry and exit of public officials suspected of duty-related crimes and their related personnel, as well as cross-border capital flows, and establish procedures for preventing flight during the investigation of cases.

## Chapter VII: Oversight over Supervisory Organs and Supervisory Personnel

**Article 60** Supervision commissions at all levels shall accept the oversight of the people's congress at the corresponding level and its standing committee.

The standing committees of people's congresses at all levels shall hear and deliberate special work reports of supervision commissions at the corresponding level and organize law enforcement inspections.

When the people's congress at or above the county level or its standing committee is in session, deputies to the people's congress or members of the standing committee may, in accordance with the procedures prescribed by law, raise inquiries or interpellations on relevant issues in supervision work.

**Article 61** Supervisory organs shall disclose supervision work information in accordance with the law and accept democratic oversight, public oversight, and media oversight.

**Article 62** Supervisory organs may, as required by work, engage special supervisory officers from representatives of various sectors. Special supervisory officers shall, in accordance with regulations, oversee the performance of duties by supervisory organs and their staff.

**Article 63** Supervisory organs shall, through the establishment of internal specialized supervision organs and other means, strengthen the oversight of supervisory personnel's performance of duties and compliance with laws, and build a supervisory team that is loyal, clean, and responsible.

**Article 64** Where a supervisory person is suspected of serious duty-related violations or duty-related crimes, the supervisory organ may, upon approval in accordance with the law and to prevent more serious consequences or adverse effects, take the measure of confinement against that person. The period of confinement shall not exceed seven days.

A person under confinement shall cooperate with the investigation by the supervisory organ. Where, after investigation, the supervisory organ finds that the confined person meets the conditions for protective custody or custodial detention, it may take such measures against that person.

The provisions of Article 50 of this Law shall apply to the measure of confinement.

**Article 65** Supervisory personnel must faithfully observe the Constitution and laws, be loyal to their duties, enforce the law impartially, be honest and clean, and maintain confidentiality. They must possess good political qualities, be familiar with supervisory work, have the ability to apply laws, regulations, and policies and to conduct investigations and collect evidence, and consciously accept oversight.

**Article 66** Where supervisory personnel inquire about case details, intercede in cases, or intervene through personal connections regarding a case, the supervisory personnel handling the supervision matter shall promptly report it. The relevant circumstances shall be registered and filed.

Where a person discovers that a supervisory person handling a supervision matter has, without approval, contacted the person under investigation, a person involved in the case, or their specific related persons, or has engaged in interactions with them, the person who knows of this shall promptly report it. The relevant circumstances shall be registered and filed.

**Article 67** Where a supervisory person handling a supervision matter falls under any of the following circumstances, they shall recuse themselves, and the supervisee, the informant, and other relevant persons shall also have the right to request their recusal:

(1) Being a close relative of the supervisee or the informant;

(2) Having served as a witness in the case;

(3) The person themselves or their close relatives have an interest in the supervision matter being handled;

(4) Other circumstances that may affect the fair handling of the supervision matter.

**Article 68** After leaving their posts, supervisory personnel who have access to classified information shall comply with the management provisions on the period of confidentiality, strictly fulfill their confidentiality obligations, and shall not disclose relevant secrets.

Within three years after resigning or retiring, supervisory personnel shall not engage in professions related to supervision and judicial work that may give rise to a conflict of interest.

**Article 69** Where a supervisory organ or its staff commits any of the following acts, the person under investigation and their close relatives or interested parties shall have the right to file a complaint with the organ:

(1) Failing to release or change the measures of compulsory appearance, ordered placement under surveillance, protective custody, custodial detention, or confinement upon expiration of the statutory period;

(2) Sealing, seizing, or freezing property unrelated to the case or clearly beyond the scope of the case;

(3) Failing to lift sealing, seizure, or freezing measures when they should be lifted;

(4) Embezzling, misappropriating, privately distributing, substituting, or using in violation of regulations the property sealed, seized, or frozen;

(5) Unlawfully interfering with the production and operation of enterprises through abuse of power or infringing upon the personal rights, property rights, and other lawful rights and interests of enterprise operators;

(6) Other violations of laws and regulations that infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of the person under investigation.

The supervisory organ that receives the complaint shall make a handling decision within one month from the date of receipt of the complaint. Where the complainant disagrees with the handling decision, they may, within one month from the date of receipt of the handling decision, apply for review to the supervisory organ at the next higher level. The supervisory organ at the next higher level shall make a handling decision within two months from the date of receipt of the review application. Where the circumstances are verified, the matter shall be promptly corrected.

**Article 70** Where, after the conclusion of the investigation, it is discovered that the basis for filing the case was insufficient or untrue, there was a major error in the case disposition, or supervisory personnel have committed serious violations of law, the responsibility of the leading persons and directly responsible persons with responsibility shall be pursued.

## Chapter VIII: Legal Liability

**Article 71** Where a relevant unit refuses to execute a disposition decision made by a supervisory organ, or without justifiable reasons refuses to adopt a supervision recommendation, the competent department or the organ at the next higher level shall order correction and circulate a notice of criticism of the unit. The leading persons and directly responsible persons with responsibility shall be dealt with in accordance with the law.

**Article 72** Where a relevant person violates the provisions of this Law by committing any of the following acts, the unit to which they belong, the competent department, the organ at the next higher level, or the supervisory organ shall order correction and handle the matter in accordance with the law:

(1) Failing to provide relevant materials as required, or refusing or obstructing the implementation of investigation measures, and otherwise refusing to cooperate with the investigation of the supervisory organ;

(2) Providing false information to conceal the truth;

(3) Colluding in confessions or forging, concealing, or destroying evidence;

(4) Preventing others from exposing, reporting, or providing evidence;

(5) Other acts in violation of the provisions of this Law, where the circumstances are serious.

**Article 73** Where a supervisee retaliates against or frames an accuser, informant, witness, or supervisory personnel; or where an accuser, informant, or witness fabricates facts to falsely accuse or frame a supervisee, the matter shall be dealt with in accordance with the law.

**Article 74** Where a supervisory organ or its staff commits any of the following acts, the leading persons and directly responsible persons with responsibility shall be dealt with in accordance with the law:

(1) Disposing of leads without approval or authorization, concealing and failing to report significant case developments, or privately retaining or handling case-related materials;

(2) Interfering with investigation work or using cases for personal gain through the abuse of power or official influence;

(3) Illegally obtaining or disclosing investigation information, or disclosing tip-off matters, the status of tip-off receipt, or informant information;

(4) Coercing confessions or inducing confessions from the person under investigation or persons involved in the case, or insulting, physically assaulting, abusing, physically punishing, or covertly physically punishing them;

(5) Disposing of sealed, seized, or frozen property in violation of regulations;

(6) Causing safety incidents in case handling in violation of regulations, or concealing, falsely reporting, or improperly handling the incident after it occurs;

(7) Taking the measures of compulsory appearance, ordered placement under surveillance, protective custody, custodial detention, or confinement in violation of regulations, or failing to release or change such measures upon expiration of the statutory period;

(8) Taking technical investigation or exit restriction measures in violation of regulations, or failing to lift technical investigation or exit restriction measures in accordance with regulations;

(9) Unlawfully interfering with the production and operation of enterprises through abuse of power or infringing upon the personal rights, property rights, and other lawful rights and interests of enterprise operators;

(10) Other acts of abusing power, neglecting duties, or engaging in favoritism and fraud.

**Article 75** Where a violation of the provisions of this Law constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be pursued in accordance with the law.

**Article 76** Where a supervisory organ or its staff, in exercising their functions and powers, infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of citizens, legal persons, or other organizations and causes damage, state compensation shall be given in accordance with the law.

## Chapter IX: Supplementary Provisions

**Article 77** The specific provisions for supervision work in the Chinese People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall be formulated by the Central Military Commission in accordance with this Law.

**Article 78** This Law shall come into force on the date of its promulgation. The Administrative Supervision Law of the People's Republic of China shall be simultaneously abolished.
