# Law of the People's Republic of China on Administrative Discipline for Public Officials (2020.6.20)

## Content

```text
Law of the People's Republic of China on Administrative Discipline for Public Officials

(Adopted at the 19th Meeting of the Standing Committee of the 13th National People's Congress on June 20, 2020)

Table of Contents

Chapter I   General Provisions

Chapter II   Types and Application of Administrative Discipline

Chapter III   Illegal Acts and Applicable Administrative Discipline

Chapter IV   Procedures for Administrative Discipline

Chapter V   Review and Reconsideration

Chapter VI   Legal Liability

Chapter VII   Supplementary Provisions

Chapter I   General Provisions

Article 1 This Law is enacted in accordance with the Supervision Law of the People's Republic of China, for the purposes of regulating administrative discipline, strengthening supervision over all public officials who exercise public power, and promoting public officials' lawful performance of duties, impartial exercise of power, clean governance in official and professional activities, and adherence to moral integrity.

Article 2 This Law applies to the activities of supervisory organs in imposing administrative discipline upon public officials who have violated the law.

Chapters II and III of this Law apply to the imposition of sanctions by the appointment and removal authorities and units of public officials upon public officials who have violated the law. The procedures for sanctions, appeals, and other matters shall be governed by other laws, administrative regulations, departmental rules of the State Council, and relevant state provisions.

"Public officials" as used in this Law refers to the persons specified in Article 15 of the Supervision Law of the People's Republic of China.

Article 3 Supervisory organs shall, in accordance with their management authority, strengthen supervision over public officials and impose administrative discipline upon public officials who violate the law according to law.

The appointment and removal authorities and units of public officials shall, in accordance with their management authority, strengthen the education, management, and supervision of public officials, and impose sanctions upon public officials who violate the law according to law.

Where a supervisory organ discovers that the appointment and removal authority or unit of a public official should have imposed a sanction but failed to do so, or has imposed an illegal or inappropriate sanction, it shall promptly make a supervisory recommendation.

Article 4 The imposition of administrative discipline upon public officials shall adhere to the principle of Party management of cadres and decisions made through collective discussion; adhere to the principle of equality before the law, based on facts and taking law as the criterion, ensuring that the administrative discipline imposed is commensurate with the nature, circumstances, and degree of harm of the illegal act; and adhere to the combination of punishment and education, with leniency and severity being applied appropriately.

Article 5 The imposition of administrative discipline upon public officials shall be based on clear facts, conclusive evidence, accurate characterization, appropriate handling, lawful procedures, and complete formalities.

Article 6 Public officials' lawful performance of duties shall be protected by law, and no administrative discipline shall be imposed except for statutory causes and through statutory procedures.

Chapter II   Types and Application of Administrative Discipline

Article 7 The types of administrative discipline are:

(1) Warning;
(2) Demerit;
(3) Serious demerit;
(4) Demotion in rank;
(5) Removal from office;
(6) Dismissal.

Article 8 The duration of administrative discipline shall be:

(1) Warning, six months;
(2) Demerit, twelve months;
(3) Serious demerit, eighteen months;
(4) Demotion in rank and removal from office, twenty-four months.

The decision on administrative discipline shall take effect on the date it is made, and the term of administrative discipline shall be calculated from the date the decision on administrative discipline takes effect.

Article 9 Where two or more public officials jointly commit an illegal act, administrative discipline shall be imposed on each respectively according to their respective roles in the illegal act and the legal liability they shall bear.

Article 10 Where a relevant authority, unit, or organization makes a collective decision that is illegal or commits an illegal act, administrative discipline shall be imposed according to law on the public officials among the responsible leaders and the directly responsible personnel.

Article 11 Administrative discipline may be imposed leniently or mitigated in any of the following circumstances:

(1) Voluntarily confessing one's own illegal act for which administrative discipline should be imposed;
(2) Cooperating with the investigation and truthfully explaining the facts of one's own illegal act;
(3) Reporting another person's disciplinary or illegal act, which is verified to be true;
(4) Voluntarily taking measures to effectively avoid, recoup losses, or eliminate adverse effects;
(5) Playing a secondary or auxiliary role in a joint illegal act;
(6) Voluntarily surrendering or returning illegal gains;
(7) Other circumstances for leniency or mitigation provided by laws or regulations.

Article 12 Where the illegal act of a public official is minor and falls under any of the circumstances provided in Article 11 of this Law, the public official may be subject to a talk and reminder, criticism and education, ordered to make an inspection, or given an admonishment, and may be exempted from or not subjected to administrative discipline.

Where a public official who, being unaware of the truth or coerced, participated in illegal activities and demonstrates genuine repentance after criticism and education, the administrative discipline may be mitigated, or the public official may be exempted from or not subjected to administrative discipline.

Article 13 Administrative discipline shall be imposed more severely in any of the following circumstances:

(1) Intentionally committing another illegal act during the term of administrative discipline for which administrative discipline should be imposed;
(2) Preventing others from reporting or providing evidence;
(3) Colluding to provide consistent statements or forging, concealing, or destroying evidence;
(4) Harboring co-offenders;
(5) Coercing or instigating others to commit illegal acts;
(6) Refusing to surrender or return illegal gains;
(7) Other circumstances for aggravated imposition provided by laws or regulations.

Article 14 Where a public official commits a crime and falls under any of the following circumstances, they shall be dismissed:

(1) Sentenced to public surveillance, criminal detention, or fixed-term imprisonment or a more severe punishment (including probation) for an intentional crime;
(2) Sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment for a term exceeding three years for a negligent crime;
(3) Sentenced to deprivation of political rights as a sole or supplementary punishment for a crime.

Where a public official is sentenced to public surveillance, criminal detention, or fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years for a negligent crime, they shall generally be dismissed; where the circumstances of the case are special and removal from office is more appropriate, they may not be dismissed, but approval from the authority at the next higher level shall be obtained.

Where a public official is sentenced to a fine only for a crime, or where the circumstances of the crime are minor and the People's Procuratorate makes a decision not to prosecute according to law or the People's Court exempts from criminal punishment according to law, they shall be removed from office; where adverse effects are caused, they shall be dismissed.

Article 15 Where a public official has committed two or more illegal acts, the administrative discipline for each act shall be determined separately. Where two or more types of administrative discipline should be imposed, the heaviest one shall be enforced; where multiple identical administrative disciplines below removal from office should be imposed, the term of administrative discipline may be determined as a period not less than one term of administrative discipline and not more than the sum of multiple terms, but the maximum shall not exceed forty-eight months.

Article 16 For the same illegal act committed by a public official, the supervisory organ and the appointment and removal authority or unit of the public official shall not repeatedly impose administrative discipline and sanctions.

Article 17 Where a public official commits an illegal act and the relevant authority imposes organizational measures in accordance with regulations, the supervisory organ may simultaneously impose administrative discipline.

Article 18 Where a public official holding a leadership position commits an illegal act and is removed from, relieved of, dismissed from, or resigns from the leadership position, the supervisory organ may simultaneously impose administrative discipline.

Article 19 Civil servants and personnel managed by reference to the Civil Servant Law of the People's Republic of China shall not be promoted in position, rank, military or police rank, or grade during the term of administrative discipline; among them, those who have received a demerit, serious demerit, demotion in rank, or removal from office shall not be promoted in salary grade. Those who are removed from office shall have their position, rank, military or police rank, and grade lowered in accordance with regulations, and their salary and benefits shall be reduced accordingly.

Article 20 Personnel engaged in public affairs in organizations authorized by laws or regulations or entrusted by state organs to manage public affairs according to law, as well as personnel engaged in management in publicly-run education, scientific research, culture, medical and health care, sports, and other units, shall not be promoted in position, post, staff grade, or professional title during the term of administrative discipline; among them, those who have received a demerit, serious demerit, demotion in rank, or removal from office shall not be promoted in salary and benefits grade. Those who are removed from office shall have their position, post, or staff grade lowered, and their salary and benefits shall be reduced accordingly.

Article 21 Management personnel of state-owned enterprises shall not be promoted in position, post grade, or professional title during the term of administrative discipline; among them, those who have received a demerit, serious demerit, demotion in rank, or removal from office shall not be promoted in salary and benefits grade. Those who are removed from office shall have their position or post grade lowered, and their salary and benefits shall be reduced accordingly.

Article 22 Where management personnel in grassroots self-governing mass organizations commit illegal acts, supervisory organs may impose warnings, demerits, or serious demerits.

Where management personnel in grassroots self-governing mass organizations receive administrative discipline, the people's government at the county level or township level shall reduce or withhold their subsidies and bonuses according to the specific circumstances.

Article 23 Where personnel specified in Item 6 of Article 15 of the Supervision Law of the People's Republic of China commit illegal acts, supervisory organs may impose warnings, demerits, or serious demerits. Where the circumstances are serious, the employing unit shall directly impose, or the supervisory organ shall recommend that the relevant authority or unit impose, measures such as reduction of salary and benefits, transfer from the post, or termination of personnel or labor relations.

Where personnel specified in Item 2 of Article 15 of the Supervision Law of the People's Republic of China do not hold positions as civil servants, personnel managed by reference to the Civil Servant Law of the People's Republic of China, personnel of public institutions, or personnel of state-owned enterprises, their illegal acts shall be dealt with in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

Article 24 A public official who has been dismissed, or who has been subjected to termination of personnel or labor relations in accordance with Article 23 of this Law, shall not be employed as a civil servant or as personnel managed by reference to the Civil Servant Law of the People's Republic of China.

Article 25 The property illegally obtained by a public official through illegal acts and the public official's own property used for illegal acts shall, unless it should be confiscated, recovered, or ordered to be returned by other authorities according to law, be confiscated, recovered, or ordered to be returned by the supervisory organ; where it should be returned to the original owner or holder, it shall be returned according to law; where it is state property or should not be returned or cannot be returned, it shall be turned over to the state treasury.

Where a public official has obtained other benefits such as positions, ranks, military or police ranks, grades, posts and staff grades, professional titles, treatment, qualifications, educational degrees, academic degrees, honors, or awards as a result of illegal acts, the supervisory organ shall recommend that the relevant authority, unit, or organization make corrections in accordance with regulations.

Article 26 Where a public official is dismissed, the personnel or labor relations with the authority or unit where the public official works shall be terminated from the date the decision on administrative discipline takes effect.

Where a public official receives administrative discipline other than dismissal and demonstrates repentance during the term of administrative discipline without committing any further illegal act requiring administrative discipline, the administrative discipline shall be automatically lifted upon expiration of the term, and promotion in position, rank, military or police rank, grade, post and staff grade, professional title, and salary and benefits shall no longer be affected by the original administrative discipline. However, those whose demotion in rank or removal from office has been lifted shall not have their original positions, ranks, military or police ranks, grades, posts and staff grades, professional titles, or salary and benefits restored.

Article 27 Where a public official who has already retired commits an illegal act before or after retirement, administrative discipline shall no longer be imposed, but an investigation may be initiated; where demotion in rank, removal from office, or dismissal should be imposed according to law, the treatment enjoyed by the public official shall be adjusted accordingly in accordance with regulations, and the property illegally obtained and the public official's own property used for the illegal act shall be dealt with in accordance with Article 25 of this Law.

Where a public official who has already left office or died committed an illegal act during the performance of duties, the matter shall be dealt with in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

Chapter III   Illegal Acts and Applicable Administrative Discipline

Article 28 The commission of any of the following acts shall result in a demerit or serious demerit; where the circumstances are relatively serious, demotion in rank or removal from office; where the circumstances are serious, dismissal:

(1) Disseminating statements that undermine the authority of the Constitution, the leadership of the Communist Party of China, or the reputation of the state;
(2) Participating in assemblies, processions, demonstrations, or other activities aimed at opposing the Constitution, the leadership of the Communist Party of China, or the state;
(3) Refusing to implement or covertly failing to implement the lines, policies, and major decisions and deployments of the Communist Party of China and the state;
(4) Participating in illegal organizations or illegal activities;
(5) Inciting or undermining ethnic relations, or participating in ethnic separatist activities;
(6) Using religious activities to undermine ethnic unity and social stability;
(7) Damaging national honor and interests in foreign exchanges.

Where any of the acts specified in Items 2, 4, 5, and 6 of the preceding paragraph is committed, the planners, organizers, and core members shall be dismissed.

Those who publicly publish articles, speeches, declarations, or statements opposing the guiding ideology established by the Constitution for the state, opposing the leadership of the Communist Party of China, opposing the socialist system, or opposing reform and opening up shall be dismissed.

Article 29 Failure to request instructions or report major matters in accordance with regulations, where the circumstances are relatively serious, shall result in a warning, demerit, or serious demerit; where the circumstances are serious, demotion in rank or removal from office.

Where provisions on reporting personal matters are violated by concealing and failing to report, and the circumstances are relatively serious, a warning, demerit, or serious demerit shall be imposed.

Tampering with or forging one's own personnel files shall result in a demerit or serious demerit; where the circumstances are serious, demotion in rank or removal from office.

Article 30 The commission of any of the following acts shall result in a warning, demerit, or serious demerit; where the circumstances are serious, demotion in rank or removal from office:

(1) Violating the principle of democratic centralism by having an individual or a minority decide major matters, or refusing to implement or arbitrarily changing a major decision made collectively;
(2) Refusing to implement, covertly failing to implement, or delaying the implementation of a decision or order lawfully made by a higher authority.

Article 31 Leaving the country or obtaining a passport for private overseas travel in violation of regulations shall result in a demerit or serious demerit; where the circumstances are serious, demotion in rank or removal from office.

Obtaining foreign nationality or acquiring the qualification for permanent overseas residence or a long-term residence permit in violation of regulations shall result in removal from office or dismissal.

Article 32 The commission of any of the following acts shall result in a warning, demerit, or serious demerit; where the circumstances are relatively serious, demotion in rank or removal from office; where the circumstances are serious, dismissal:

(1) Violating relevant regulations in personnel work such as selection and appointment, recruitment, employment, assessment, promotion, and evaluation;
(2) Engaging in fraud to obtain positions, ranks, military or police ranks, grades, posts and staff grades, professional titles, treatment, qualifications, educational degrees, academic degrees, honors, awards, or other benefits;
(3) Suppressing or retaliating against persons who lawfully exercise rights such as criticism, appeal, complaint, or reporting;
(4) Making false accusations with the intent to harm another person's reputation or cause accountability investigation or other adverse effects;
(5) Disrupting elections through violence, threats, bribery, deception, or other means.

Article 33 The commission of any of the following acts shall result in a warning, demerit, or serious demerit; where the circumstances are relatively serious, demotion in rank or removal from office; where the circumstances are serious, dismissal:

(1) Engaging in embezzlement or bribery;
(2) Using one's position or the influence of one's position to seek private gain for oneself or others;
(3) Indulging or acquiescing to a specifically related person using one's position or the influence of one's position to seek private gain.

Where one refuses to rectify, in accordance with regulations, a specifically related person'sviolation concurrent appointment, part-time position, or business activities and does not comply with position adjustment, removal from office shall be imposed.

Article 34 Accepting gifts, cash gifts, marketable securities, or other property that may affect the impartial exercise of public power shall result in a warning, demerit, or serious demerit; where the circumstances are relatively serious, demotion in rank or removal from office; where the circumstances are serious, dismissal.

Where gifts, cash gifts, marketable securities, or other property that may affect the impartial exercise of public power are given to public officials or their specifically related persons, or where entertainment, travel, fitness, recreational, or other activity arrangements that may affect the impartial exercise of public power are accepted or provided, and the circumstances are relatively serious, a warning, demerit, or serious demerit shall be imposed; where the circumstances are serious, demotion in rank or removal from office.

Article 35 The commission of any of the following acts, where the circumstances are relatively serious, shall result in a warning, demerit, or serious demerit; where the circumstances are serious, demotion in rank or removal from office:

(1) Setting or distributing salaries, allowances, subsidies, or bonuses in violation of regulations;
(2) Exceeding standards or scope, in violation of regulations, in official receptions, official transportation, meetings and activities, office space, and other work and living support;
(3) Using public funds for consumption in violation of regulations.

Article 36 Engaging in or participating in for-profit activities in violation of regulations, or holding concurrent positions or receiving remuneration in violation of regulations, shall result in a warning, demerit, or serious demerit; where the circumstances are relatively serious, demotion in rank or removal from office; where the circumstances are serious, dismissal.

Article 37 Using clan or mafia-style criminal syndicates or other evil forces to oppress the masses, or indulging or harboring the activities of mafia-style criminal syndicates or evil forces, shall result in removal from office; where the circumstances are serious, dismissal.

Article 38 The commission of any of the following acts, where the circumstances are relatively serious, shall result in a warning, demerit, or serious demerit; where the circumstances are serious, demotion in rank or removal from office:

(1) Collecting or apportioning property from management or service targets in violation of regulations;
(2) Deliberately creating difficulties, extorting, or demanding kickbacks in management or service activities;
(3) Displaying a harsh and rude attitude in management or service activities, causing adverse consequences or effects;
(4) Failing to publicly disclose work information in accordance with regulations, infringing upon the right to know of management or service targets, causing adverse consequences or effects;
(5) Other acts infringing upon the interests of management or service targets, causing adverse consequences or effects.

Where the acts in Items 1, 2, and 5 of the preceding paragraph are committed and the circumstances are especially serious, dismissal shall be imposed.

Article 39 The commission of any of the following acts, causing adverse consequences or effects, shall result in a warning, demerit, or serious demerit; where the circumstances are relatively serious, demotion in rank or removal from office; where the circumstances are serious, dismissal:

(1) Abusing power, harming national interests, social public interests, or infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of citizens, legal persons, or other organizations;
(2) Failing to perform or correctly performing duties, neglecting duties, or delaying work;
(3) Engaging in formalism or bureaucracy in work;
(4) Engaging in fraud, misleading, or deception in work;
(5) Disclosing state secrets or work secrets, or disclosing trade secrets or personal privacy learned through the performance of duties.

Article 40 The commission of any of the following acts shall result in a warning, demerit, or serious demerit; where the circumstances are relatively serious, demotion in rank or removal from office; where the circumstances are serious, dismissal:

(1) Violating public order and good customs by engaging in improper conduct in public places, causing adverse effects;
(2) Participating in or supporting superstitious activities, causing adverse effects;
(3) Participating in gambling;
(4) Refusing to bear the duty of support, maintenance, or foster care;
(5) Committing domestic violence, abusing, or abandoning family members;
(6) Other acts that seriously violate family virtues or social ethics.

Ingesting or injecting drugs, organizing gambling, or organizing, supporting, or participating in prostitution, solicitation of prostitution, or obscene and promiscuous activities shall result in removal from office or dismissal.

Article 41 Where a public official commits other illegal acts that affect the image of public officials and harm the interests of the state and the people, corresponding administrative discipline may be imposed according to the severity of the circumstances.

Chapter IV   Procedures for Administrative Discipline

Article 42 Investigations by supervisory organs into public officials suspected of illegal acts shall be conducted by two or more staff members. When conducting investigations, supervisory organs have the right to learn about the circumstances from relevant units and individuals and to collect and obtain evidence according to law. Relevant units and individuals shall truthfully provide information.

It is strictly prohibited to collect evidence through threats, inducements, deception, or other illegal means. Evidence collected through illegal means shall not be used as a basis for imposing administrative discipline.

Article 43 Before making a decision on administrative discipline, the supervisory organ shall inform the person under investigation of the illegal facts identified through investigation and the basis for the proposed administrative discipline, hear the person's statements and defense, verify the facts, reasons, and evidence presented, and record them in the case file. Facts, reasons, and evidence presented by the person under investigation that are established shall be accepted. The administrative discipline shall not be aggravated because of the person under investigation's defense.

Article 44 After the conclusion of the investigation, the supervisory organ shall handle the matter separately according to the following different circumstances:

(1) Where there is indeed an illegal act for which administrative discipline should be imposed, a decision on administrative discipline shall be made after completing the prescribed formalities for approval in accordance with the authority for making administrative discipline decisions and based on the severity of the circumstances;
(2) Where the facts of the illegal act cannot be established, the case shall be dismissed;
(3) Where the conditions for exemption from or non-imposition of administrative discipline are met, a decision on exemption from or non-imposition of administrative discipline shall be made;
(4) Where the person under investigation is suspected of other illegal or criminal acts, the matter shall be transferred to the competent authority for handling according to law.

Article 45 Where a decision is made to impose administrative discipline, a written decision on administrative discipline shall be prepared.

The written decision on administrative discipline shall specify the following matters:

(1) The name, work unit, and position of the person subjected to the discipline;
(2) The facts of the illegal act and the evidence;
(3) The type of administrative discipline and the legal basis;
(4) The channels and time limit for applying for review or reconsideration if the person disagrees with the administrative discipline decision;
(5) The name of the authority that made the administrative discipline decision and the date.

The written decision on administrative discipline shall bear the seal of the supervisory organ that made the decision.

Article 46 The written decision on administrative discipline shall be promptly served upon the person subjected to the discipline and the authority or unit where the person works, and shall be announced within a certain scope.

After making a decision on administrative discipline, the supervisory organ shall notify the relevant authority or unit in writing based on the specific identity of the person subjected to the discipline.

Article 47 Personnel participating in the investigation and handling of illegal cases involving public officials shall voluntarily withdraw in any of the following circumstances, and the person under investigation, the reporter, and other relevant persons also have the right to request their withdrawal:

(1) Being a close relative of the person under investigation or the reporter;
(2) Having served as a witness in the case;
(3) Having an interest in the case under investigation themselves or through a close relative;
(4) Other circumstances that may affect the impartial investigation and handling of the case.

Article 48 The withdrawal of the head of a supervisory organ shall be decided by the supervisory organ at the next higher level; the withdrawal of other personnel participating in the investigation and handling of illegal cases shall be decided by the head of the supervisory organ.

Where a supervisory organ or the supervisory organ at the next higher level discovers that personnel participating in the investigation and handling of an illegal case have a circumstance requiring withdrawal, it may directly decide on the withdrawal of such personnel.

Article 49 Where a public official is subject to criminal liability according to law, the supervisory organ shall impose administrative discipline in accordance with the provisions of this Law based on the effective judgments, rulings, or decisions of the judicial organs and the facts and circumstances determined therein.

Where a public official is subject to administrative punishment according to law and should be given administrative discipline, the supervisory organ may impose administrative discipline in accordance with this Law after initiating an investigation, verifying the facts and circumstances determined in the administrative punishment decision.

Where, after the supervisory organ makes a decision on administrative discipline in accordance with the provisions of paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article, the judicial or administrative authority changes the original effective judgment, ruling, decision, or the like according to law, and this affects the original administrative discipline decision, the supervisory organ shall make a corresponding new handling decision based on the changed judgment, ruling, decision, or the like.

Article 50 Where a supervisory organ removes from office or dismisses a public official who has been elected or appointed by decision through the People's Congresses at various levels or the Standing Committees of the People's Congresses at or above the county level, the supervisory organ shall first have the person's position recalled, revoked, or removed according to law, and then make a decision on administrative discipline according to law.

Where a supervisory organ removes from office or dismisses a public official who has been elected or appointed by decision through the plenary sessions of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference (CPPCC) committees at various levels or their standing committees, the supervisory organ shall first have the person's position removed in accordance with theconstitutional documents, and then make a decision on administrative discipline according to law.

Where a supervisory organ imposes administrative discipline on deputies to the People's Congresses at various levels or members of CPPCC committees at various levels, it shall notify the relevant Standing Committee of the People's Congress, the presidium of the People's Congress of a township, ethnic township, or town, or the Standing Committee of the CPPCC committee.

Article 51 Where a lower-level supervisory organ investigates a case pursuant to a decision on designated jurisdiction made by a higher-level supervisory organ, after the conclusion of the investigation, if the subject of supervision is not within the jurisdiction of the investigating supervisory organ, the case shall be transferred to the supervisory organ with management authority to make a decision on administrative discipline according to law.

Article 52 Where a public official suspected of illegal acts has been placed on file for investigation and is not suitable to continue performing duties, the public official's appointment and removal authority or unit may decide to suspend the performance of duties.

While a public official is under investigation on a filed case, the public official shall not leave the country or resign from public office without the consent of the supervisory organ; the authority or unit where the public official under investigation works and the higher-level authority or unit shall not arrange for transfer, promotion, reward, sanction, or handle retirement procedures for the public official.

Article 53 Where, during an investigation, a supervisory organ discovers that a public official has been subjected to false reports, accusations, or false denunciations causing adverse effects, the supervisory organ shall, in accordance with regulations, promptly clarify the facts, restore the person's reputation, and eliminate adverse effects.

Article 54 Where a public official receives administrative discipline, the written decision on administrative discipline shall be placed in the public official's personnel file. For those who receive administrative discipline at or above the level of demotion in rank, the personnel department shall, in accordance with the management authority, handle the procedures for changing the position, salary, and other related benefits within one month after the decision on administrative discipline is made; under special circumstances, the time limit for handling may be appropriately extended with approval, but the maximum extension shall not exceed six months.

Chapter V   Review and Reconsideration

Article 55 A public official who disagrees with a decision on administrative discipline made by a supervisory organ concerning themselves may apply to the supervisory organ that made the decision for review according to law; a public official who still disagrees with the review decision may apply to the supervisory organ at the next higher level for reconsideration.

Where a supervisory organ discovers that a decision on administrative discipline made by itself or a lower-level supervisory organ is indeed erroneous, it shall promptly correct it or order the lower-level supervisory organ to promptly correct it.

Article 56 During the period of review and reconsideration, the execution of the original decision on administrative discipline shall not be suspended.

A public official shall not receive a heavier administrative discipline because they applied for review or reconsideration.

Article 57 In any of the following circumstances, the review or reconsideration authority shall revoke the original decision on administrative discipline and make a new decision or order the supervisory organ that made the original decision to make a new decision:

(1) The facts of the illegal act on which the administrative discipline is based are unclear or the evidence is insufficient;
(2) The legal procedures are violated, affecting the impartial handling of the case;
(3) The decision on administrative discipline was made by exceeding authority or abusing power.

Article 58 In any of the following circumstances, the review or reconsideration authority shall modify the original decision on administrative discipline, or order the supervisory organ that made the original decision to modify it:

(1) The application of laws or regulations is indeed erroneous;
(2) The determination of the circumstances of the illegal act is indeed erroneous;
(3) The administrative discipline is inappropriate.

Article 59 Where the review or reconsideration authority finds that the facts determined in the administrative discipline decision are clear and the applicable law is correct, it shall uphold the decision.

Article 60 Where a decision on administrative discipline concerning a public official is modified and it is necessary to adjust the public official's position, rank, military or police rank, grade, post and staff grade, or salary and benefits, the adjustment shall be made in accordance with regulations. Where the decision on administrative discipline is revoked, the public official's grade, salary, and benefits shall be restored, corresponding positions, ranks, military or police ranks, posts, and staff grades shall be arranged according to the original position, rank, military or police rank, post, and staff grade, and the public official's reputation shall be restored within the scope in which the original decision on administrative discipline was announced. Where the confiscated or recovered property was handled erroneously, it shall be returned or compensated according to law.

Where a public official's administrative discipline is revoked or mitigated due to the circumstances specified in Articles 57 or 58 of this Law, the loss of salary and benefits suffered by the public official shall be compensated.

Chapter VI   Legal Liability

Article 61 Where a relevant authority or unit refuses to adopt a supervisory recommendation without justifiable reasons, the authority at the next higher level or the competent department shall order it to make corrections, issue a notice of criticism to the authority or unit, and handle the responsible leaders and directly responsible personnel according to law.

Article 62 Where a relevant authority, unit, organization, or person falls under any of the following circumstances, the authority at the next higher level, the competent department, the appointment and removal authority or unit, or the supervisory organ shall order corrections and handle the matter according to law:

(1) Refusing to execute a decision on administrative discipline;
(2) Refusing to cooperate with or obstructing an investigation;
(3) Retaliating against a reporter, witness, or investigator;
(4) Making false accusations against public officials;
(5) Other circumstances violating the provisions of this Law.

Article 63 Where a supervisory organ or its staff members fall under any of the following circumstances, the responsible leaders and directly responsible personnel shall be handled according to law:

(1) Handling clues of problems in violation of regulations;
(2) Stealing or disclosing investigation work information, or disclosing reported matters, the receipt of reports, or information about the reporter;
(3) Forcing confessions or inducing statements from the person under investigation or the persons involved in the case, or insulting, beating, abusing, physically punishing, or covertly physically punishing them;
(4) Accepting property or other benefits from the person under investigation or the persons involved in the case;
(5) Disposing of property involved in the case in violation of regulations;
(6) Adopting investigation measures in violation of regulations;
(7) Using one's position or the influence of one's position to interfere with investigation work or seeking personal gain through casework;
(8) Causing safety incidents in case handling due to violations of regulations, or concealing, misreporting, or improperly handling incidents after their occurrence;
(9) Violating procedural provisions such as withdrawal, causing adverse effects;
(10) Failing to accept and handle reviews and reconsiderations by public officials according to law;
(11) Other acts of abusing power, neglecting duties, or engaging in favoritism and fraud.

Article 64 Where any provision of this Law is violated and a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be pursued according to law.

Chapter VII   Supplementary Provisions

Article 65 The State Council and its relevant competent departments shall, in accordance with the principles and spirit of this Law and in light of the actual circumstances of public institutions, state-owned enterprises, and other entities, make specific provisions on matters concerning the sanctions against public officials in public institutions, state-owned enterprises, and other entities who have violated the law.

Article 66 The Central Military Commission may formulate relevant specific provisions in accordance with this Law.

Article 67 Where a case that has been concluded before the implementation of this Law requires review or reconsideration, the provisions in force at the time shall apply. For cases that have not yet been concluded, if the provisions in force at the time the act was committed did not consider it to be illegal, the provisions in force at the time shall apply; if the provisions in force at the time the act was committed considered it to be illegal, it shall be dealt with according to the provisions in force at the time, except that if this Law does not consider it to be illegal or imposes a lighter sanction, this Law shall apply.

Article 68 This Law shall come into effect on July 1, 2020.
```
